BUSINESS OVERVIEW AND STRATEGY

focussed on ensuring a seamless omni-channel experience for customers and are the second largest digital-first omni-channel jewellery brand in India, in terms of revenues in Fiscal 2024. We retail 
our products through our website www.bluestone.com and our mobile application available on iOS and Google Play Store, in addition to our pan-India network of stores. We are among the few 
Leading Jewellery Retailers* with a pan-India presence with 275 stores across 117 cities in 26 States and Union Territories in India, as of March 31, 2025 servicing over 12,600 PIN codes across India.
