# Restatement register — BSE Limited

**Method.** For each annual report, the right-hand column is the prior year *as that AR now presents
it*. Compared mechanically against the left-hand column of the prior year's own AR (*as first
reported*). Generated by `detect_restatements.py`; raw output in `restatements.csv` / `.json`.

**Why this matters.** Ten-year trend lines built by taking each year from its own AR are **not
comparable** where a restatement intervened. Growth computed across a restatement boundary is wrong in
a direction that flatters or deflates depending on which side you stand on.

## Status legend
`VERIFIED` — re-read on the cited page, both sides. `ARTIFACT` — a measurement error in the detector,
not a restatement. `CANDIDATE` — detected, not yet re-read.

## CORRECTED 2026-09-08 by W3_notes — the FY2018 "artifact" ALSO conceals a real restatement

The ARTIFACT ruling below is **correct for the comparison it was made against** (71,128 continuing-ops
against 21,475 total-ops) and **wrong as a conclusion that no FY2018 restatement occurred.**

There is a genuine, **stated and quantified** earnings restatement: FY2018 PAT attributable
**71,128 → 68,872 (−2,256)**, disclosed in **FY2019 Note 30 footnote 2** — ICCL Core SGF contributions
previously earmarked from investments, charged to the P&L instead.

It also part-explains the FY2018 equity movement of −5,380:
`−5,380 to Other equity, +6,752 to the Core SGF caption, +1,372 gross-up` — ties exactly.
**₹3,124 remains unexplained.**

Both positions stand, per doctrine §10. The lesson is specific: **an artifact ruling scopes only the
comparison that produced it.** Ruling a delta an artifact does not establish that the underlying line
was never restated — a separate question requiring the note, not the face.

## ARTIFACT — not restatements

`pat_attributable` FY2018 71,128 → 21,475 and `pat_total_ops` FY2018 72,453 → 70,197. The detector
matched the **continuing-operations** "Net profit attributable" line in one AR against the
**total-operations** line in the other. FY2018's own AR reports 71,128 as attributable to shareholders
including 48,722 of discontinued-operations profit `[AR FY2018, PDF p134]`. Excluded from the register.

**Detector limitation, recorded:** where an AR carries two lines with the same label (continuing vs
total operations), first-match comparison is unreliable. Only income and expense lines above the
discontinued-operations block are compared with confidence.

**FY2023 verified line by line against its own accounts** `[AR FY2023, Consolidated P&L, PDF p162]`:
revenue 81,553 · investment income 10,931 · other income 2,910 · total income 95,394 · total expenses
70,591 · PBT 29,726 · PAT attributable 22,067. All seven match the values previously recovered from
FY2024's comparative column, and the components tie: `81,553 + 10,931 + 2,910 = 95,394`.

**Why the absence is a finding, not a blank.** Four consecutive clean years (FY2020–FY2023) sit between
two restatement clusters. The restatements are not a habit — they attach to two specific events, the
CDSL deconsolidation and the FY2024 discontinued operation. That makes each one explicable rather than
symptomatic, and it is disconfirming evidence against reading the restatement register as a pattern of
accounting instability. It should be presented that way.
