# Revenue disaggregation ledger — BSE Limited

**Basis warning.** The figures below are **STANDALONE**, from the MD&A. The audited consolidated
Revenue from operations for FY2026 is **4,83,395** `[AR FY2026, Consolidated P&L, Note 22, PDF p164]`.
Standalone revenue from operations is **4,46,951** `[AR FY2026, MD&A "Financial Highlights at a
Glance (Standalone)", PDF p75]`. Any percentage-of-revenue statement must name which basis it uses.

**Source priority.** MD&A is management's presentation, below the audited note (doctrine §5).

## L2 tie-outs (DERIVED, arithmetic shown)

**Transaction charges sub-components tie exactly:**
`3,13,437 + 28,490 + 25,021 = 3,66,948` ✓ equals the reported transaction-charge line.

**Named components do NOT sum to reported revenue:**
`3,66,948 + 38,861 + 10,412 + 28,110 = 4,44,331`
`4,46,951 − 4,44,331 = 2,620` unnamed residual, **0.59%** of standalone revenue from operations.
→ L2 state for this residual: `NOT-DISCLOSED -> AR FY2026 audited Note 22 disaggregation`

**Other operating income is only 60% disaggregated:**
`28,110 − 16,899 = 11,211` not broken out, **39.9%** of that line.
`11,211 / 28,110 = 39.9%`
→ L2 state: `NOT-DISCLOSED -> AR FY2026 audited Note 22`

**Consolidated-to-standalone gap:**
`4,83,395 − 4,46,951 = 36,444` of consolidated revenue arises outside the standalone entity, **7.5%**
of consolidated revenue. Subsidiaries (ICCL, India INX, BSE Institute, BSE Technologies).
→ For W3_group_rpt: which subsidiary contributes what.

## Concentration, for W3_revenue to chain (DERIVED)

`3,13,437 / 4,46,951 = 70.1%` — equity derivatives as a share of **standalone** revenue from operations.
`3,13,437 / 4,83,395 = 64.8%` — same numerator on the **consolidated** base.

**Asymmetry to carry into S3 (doctrine §1a-i).** Within one line (transaction charges), equity
derivatives **+121%** against equity cash **−18%**. The two moved in opposite directions in the same
period, so the growth of the aggregate is not a statement about trading activity generally — it is one
product. Neither share alone shows this.

## Management's own words, for W2_promise and the story-vs-numbers test

> "Transaction charges, constituting 82% of total revenue from operations, grew 88% to ₹3,66,948 Lakh,
> powered by a transformational increase in equity derivatives activity."
> `[AR FY2026, MD&A, PDF p75]`

> "Equity cash transaction charges moderated by 18% to ₹25,021 Lakh, consistent with industry-wide
> reduction in average daily turnover value." `[AR FY2026, MD&A, PDF p75]`

> **STORY-VS-NUMBERS BREAK — verified by W2_industry.** That explanation is **contradicted by BSE's own
> volume disclosure seventeen pages earlier in the same document.** BSE's own equity-cash turnover
> **rose 2.36%** in FY2026. Equity-cash realisation fell ~19.5% (₹60.55 cr of foregone charges) on
> *rising* volume — so the fall is a **repricing, not a volume effect**. And it is not a general
> repricing either: derivatives realisation **rose 1.8%** in the same year. This is the L3-contradicts-L1
> break of doctrine §2c.2 — management's stated driver is refuted by the company's own data in the same
> annual report.

The 82% figure is on the standalone base (`3,66,948 / 4,46,951 = 82.1%` ✓). On the consolidated base it
is `3,66,948 / 4,83,395 = 75.9%`.
