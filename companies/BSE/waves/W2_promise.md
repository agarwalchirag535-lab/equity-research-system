# W2 — Promise vs delivery: the BSE management accountability tracker

**Scope.** Every specific, checkable claim made by BSE management on the record between 5-Feb-2024 and
4-Aug-2026: 11 earnings-call transcripts and 4 investor presentations. A management claim is data about
**management**, not about the business, and is scored that way throughout.

**Conclusion first.** Across 53 claims that can be scored today, management's hit rate is **61.3%**
(32.5 ÷ 53). But the aggregate hides the shape that matters. When management promises to **build**
something it controls — racks, members, FPIs, a fee schedule, a product launch date — it delivers
**65.2%** of the time (21.5 ÷ 33). When it **reassures an analyst about a number it does not control** —
a cost, a regulatory charge, a volume outcome — it is right **33.3%** of the time (4 ÷ 12). The
company's three largest P&L swings in the window (₹199cr, ₹109cr, ₹170cr = ₹478cr, 36.0% of FY25 PAT)
all landed in categories management had expressly declined to size. The promise book is almost entirely
inputs; the earnings surprises are entirely outputs.

## (b1) Two items closed against the quarterly filings

Both items the first pass left open are now settled by the Q3FY26 results filing and the Q3FY26 deck.

### J8 — the "59% from 39%" margin comparative: **management error**

Not a definitional difference and not a restatement. BSE applies one definition of Operating EBITDA
throughout: **revenue from operations less operating expenses excluding depreciation, less contribution
to core SGF**, expressed over **revenue from operations**. Both sides of the comparison tie out on that
single definition:

```
Q3FY26  12,441 - (5,112 - 448) - 456 = 7,321  ->  7,322 / 12,441 = 58.8%  -> 59%
Q3FY25   7,681 - (3,635 - 302) - 1,992 = 2,356  ->  2,356 / 7,681 = 30.7%  -> 31%
```
`[Q3FY26 Deck 09Feb2026, PDF p26, p27]`, cross-checked to the limited-reviewed statement: revenue from
operations ₹1,24,410 lakhs and ₹76,810 lakhs, total expenses ₹51,116 lakhs and ₹36,353 lakhs,
depreciation ₹4,483 lakhs and ₹3,018 lakhs, core SGF ₹4,563 lakhs and ₹19,917 lakhs
`[Q3FY26 Results 09Feb2026, PDF p5]`.

**BSE's own deck, filed the same day as the call, prints the Dec'24 comparative as 31%**
`[Q3FY26 Deck 09Feb2026, PDF p27]` — identical to what BSE reported for that quarter a year earlier
`[Q3FY25 Concall 12Feb2025, MD]`. No metric anywhere in the deck or the reviewed statements equals 39%
for Dec'24: ex-SGF margin is 57%, standalone including SGF is 47%, net profit margin is 26%
`[Q3FY26 Deck 09Feb2026, PDF p27, p29]`. The only construction reaching 38.6% requires swapping total
income for revenue from operations in the numerator while leaving revenue from operations in the
denominator, which is not a definition BSE uses anywhere and is inconsistent with the ₹236cr the MD
quoted in the same sentence.

**Two things follow, and they pull in opposite directions.** The number spoken to analysts was wrong by
8 percentage points in a headline comparative and was not corrected on the call. But the error
*understates* management's own achievement — the true expansion is 31% to 59%, +28pp, not the +20pp
claimed — and the correct figure was in the public deck the same day. This is a slip in the prepared
remarks, not a presentational choice. J8 stays scored **Missed** because the spoken claim did not hold,
but its severity is downgraded and it is no longer read as evidence of framing.

### B7 — "we restricted somewhere around 3% plus": **a definitional difference, and it is a real disclosure-consistency finding**

The two definitions, named precisely:

| | Definition | Q3FY26 value |
|---|---|---|
| **Standalone basis** — the basis the SGF policy is written on | BSE Limited's own contribution to core SGF ÷ BSE Limited's own transaction charges | ₹258mn ÷ ₹9,209mn = **2.80%** `[Q3FY26 Deck 09Feb2026, PDF p28, p29]` |
| **Consolidated basis** — group, BSE plus subsidiaries, principally ICCL | Group contribution to core SGF ÷ group transaction charges | ₹456mn ÷ ₹9,526mn = **4.79%** `[Q3FY26 Deck 09Feb2026, PDF p26, p27]` |

Management defined the policy itself as standalone, explicitly and at length: *"What we are talking
about 5% of transaction revenue is from the BSE side. Will ICCL be contributing on its own to the core
SGF? Yes. ICCL's revenue is not transaction revenue. So, we should not take ICCL contribution, add it to
BSE's contribution and put the denominator as transaction revenue and say the contribution is more than
5% of transaction revenue"* `[Q2FY26 Concall 17Nov2025, MD]`.

**My first-pass computation was wrong**: I put the consolidated numerator over the consolidated
denominator and got 4.79%, when the policy is a standalone construct. On the correct basis the
contribution rate is **2.80%**, so "somewhere around 3% plus, I guess" is broadly right in substance —
the rate was genuinely restricted below the 5% policy — and modestly overstated in the number.

**But the sentence mixes the two bases without labelling either.** In one passage the MD gives the
numerator on the consolidated basis (₹45.6cr) and the percentage on the standalone basis (2.80%). The
standalone contribution is ₹25.8cr, so the quoted numerator is 45.6 ÷ 25.8 = **1.77x** the figure the
percentage actually refers to. Both numbers are in the deck — consolidated on PDF p27, standalone on
PDF p29 — but neither the call nor the deck flags that the policy runs on one and the headline on the
other. That is the finding: the SGF policy is the single most important disclosure management added in
this window, and it is being reported on two bases in the same breath, which makes it untrackable by an
outside reader without reconstructing both P&Ls. Verdict moves **Missed to Partly delivered**.

## (d) Findings

### FINDING: Management's promise book is 62% inputs, but 100% of the P&L shocks came from the 38% it refused to size
S1 WHAT: Across 11 transcripts, 7 decks and the quarterly results filings spanning Feb-2024 to Aug-2026, BSE management issued no numeric forward guidance on revenue, margin, volumes, SGF or cost — not once. Of 53 scoreable claims, 33 (62.3%) are things BSE itself builds or announces and 20 (37.7%) are outcomes needing a market, regulator or counterparty to respond. Delivery on the first group is 65.2%; on reassurances about the second it is 33.3%.
S2 WHY: The incentive structure of an exchange under an activist regulator rewards silence on outcomes — every derivatives number is regulator-dependent and every regulator-dependent number is deniable. Management converted that into a standing rule, stated explicitly: "let me confess to you my shortcoming in not being capable of forecasting future" `[Q1FY25 Concall 13Aug2024, MD]`, and "any projection whatsoever in respect of any segment you ask, any number of times, any number of investors, the answer has to be same" `[Q3FY24 Concall 07Feb2024, MD]`. The mechanism is not caution: the same management produced a precise SGF formula the moment it chose to (5% of transaction revenue capped at 150% of MRC `[Q2FY26 Concall 17Nov2025, MD]`), proving the unknowability claim was a policy, not a constraint.
S3 EVIDENCE: 33 of 53 claims (62.3%) are input promises versus 20 of 53 (37.7%) outcome claims — but the counter-proportion is where it bites: the three largest discrete P&L items in the window, ₹199cr core SGF `[Q3FY25 Concall 12Feb2025, MD]`, ₹109cr net reversal `[Q4FY25 Concall 09May2025, MD]` and ₹170cr SEBI differential fee `[Q4FY24 Concall 13May2024, MD]`, sum to ₹478cr = 36.0% of FY25 PAT of ₹1,326cr `[Q4FY25 Concall 09May2025, MD]`, and 3 of 3 (100%) sat in the 37.7% bucket. The 62.3% of claims management made voluntarily explains 0% of the earnings variance.
S4 CONNECTS: The Income Statement shows the volatility — core SGF swung ₹2mn (Q2FY25) to ₹1,992mn (Q3FY25) to −₹1,094mn (Q4FY25) `[IP Q1FY26 07Aug2025, p26]` `[IP Q3FY25 06Feb2025, p25]`. Management commentary shows the refusals: 8 SGF projection refusals across 6 calls. Governance shows the disclosure valve: 7 quantitative questions routed offline, none answered publicly. Industry shows why the refusals were not forced — SEBI published the MRC methodology in a circular dated 3-Oct-2024 `[Q3FY25 Concall 12Feb2025, MD]`, i.e. the inputs were public and BSE modelled them internally, since IR later confirmed "it is an internal calculation, which we do on a monthly basis" `[Q2FY26 Concall 17Nov2025, IR]`. The compensating control is that BSE's execution record is genuinely good (65.2%); the class of error it cannot catch is a P&L shock in any line management has decided is unprojectable, because the control operates on inputs only.
DOMAINS: MgmtCommentary, Governance, IncomeStatement, Industry
DISCONFIRM: Searched for and found real counter-evidence. Management did give a direction ahead of the SEBI derivative reforms ("we believe these steps will impact the volumes" `[Q2FY25 Concall 19Nov2024, MD]`); it told analysts exactly how to model the regulatory fee and was right within 0.9pp (+26.1% expense vs +25.2% notional `[IP H1FY26 11Nov2025, p26, p14]`); it flagged a forward risk against its own interest ("some of the bank guarantees may mature and may not get re-issued" `[Q1FY27 Concall 10Aug2026, MD]`); and it eventually built the SGF smoothing policy it had said it was unsure it could build. Silence on outcomes is also the SEBI-consistent posture for a market infrastructure institution. The pattern is a disclosure preference, not evidence of concealment.
S5 MEANS: The disclosure record is reliable about what BSE will do and unreliable about what anything will cost, so the earnings series carries an unhedged tail that management has pre-committed not to warn about.
BASE-RATE: Realised, not theoretical. ₹478cr of actual P&L swing, 36.0% of a full year's profit, in three episodes over eight quarters — and in two of the three (B1, B2) management had specifically reassured an analyst weeks or months earlier. This has cost money at this company, twice, through this exact channel.
S6 IMPLICATION: Earnings durability should be assessed on the ex-SGF, ex-one-off operating line rather than reported PAT, and the margin of safety must absorb a ₹150-200cr unannounced charge in any quarter; the monitorable is whether the 5%/150% SGF policy survives its first real MRC step-up, with the next check the Q3FY27 call, and the thesis-breaker is a second unsized cost category emerging with the same "cannot be projected" framing.
SEVERITY: High

### FINDING: The moat management described was not the moat, and its own franchise thesis was falsified by its own action
S1 WHAT: For 16 months management attributed Sensex derivatives' success to owning an expiry day nobody else used. It then surrendered that exclusivity, moving Sensex weekly expiry onto NSE's Thursday from 1-Sep-2025, and the product immediately posted the fastest growth in its history.
S2 WHY: The real driver was never the calendar. It was the migration of volume off the expiry day into non-expiry days and longer-dated contracts, which raises premium per contract while leaving the per-contract clearing cost unchanged. BSE's revenue is charged on premium, its clearing cost on contract count and its regulatory fee on notional `[Q1FY26 Concall 13Aug2025, IR]` — so a mix shift toward fewer, fatter contracts is a pure margin event that has nothing to do with which weekday the contract dies on. Management understood the mechanism (C2, correctly predicted in Nov-2024) while simultaneously misattributing the franchise to the calendar.
S3 EVIDENCE: FY26 average daily premium turnover ₹19,523cr vs FY25 ₹8,978cr, +117.5% `[Q4FY26 Concall 13May2026, MD]` — but the counter-proportion is the volume series moving the opposite way: average daily lots fell from 153mn (Q2FY25) to 100mn (Q2FY26), −34.6% `[IP H1FY25 12Nov2024, p14]` `[IP H1FY26 11Nov2025, p14]`. Premium per lot therefore rose ₹82,034mn ÷ 153mn = ₹536 to ₹150,045mn ÷ 100mn = ₹1,500, +180%. Premium up 83% on 35% fewer contracts is the finding; neither series alone says it. Standalone clearing cost fell ₹1,244mn → ₹873mn (−29.8%) while standalone transaction charges rose ₹5,029mn → ₹7,616mn (+51.4%) over the identical two quarters `[IP H1FY26 11Nov2025, p28]`.
S4 CONNECTS: Business — the product's economics are a premium-per-contract story, not a calendar story. Management commentary — the MD conceded the calendar thesis to a bear ("No, you are perfectly right… absolutely came because it had an expiry, which did not clash" `[Q4FY24 Concall 13May2024, MD]`) and later described the Thursday choice as opportunistic rather than strategic: "we felt very strongly that Thursday is a very wonderful, great day. We did not opt for it because it was opted earlier by somebody else" `[Q1FY26 Concall 13Aug2025, MD]`. Income Statement — the margin expansion shows up as operating EBITDA margin 47% (Q1FY25) to 65% (Q1FY26) `[IP Q1FY26 07Aug2025, p27]`. Industry — SEBI's lot-size increase and one-weekly-expiry rule forced the very mix shift that created the margin, so the regulator, not the exchange, authored the improvement. The compensating control is that management's Nov-2024 prediction of the cost mechanics was correct; the class of error it cannot catch is a strategy premised on a driver management has publicly misidentified, because resource allocation follows the stated model.
DOMAINS: Business, MgmtCommentary, IncomeStatement, Industry
DISCONFIRM: Sought and partly found. The Thursday switch is confounded with rising volatility, which management cited as the swing factor in premium realisation `[Q2FY26 Concall 17Nov2025, MD]`, and Q1FY27's +96% YoY coincides with the STT and RBI-circular disruption to the peer product — so causation is not cleanly attributable to the expiry move. Equally, the calendar thesis was not baseless: when Bankex lost its exclusive weekly slot its volumes fell "almost by around 95%" `[Q3FY25 Concall 12Feb2025, MD]`. The honest reading is that calendar exclusivity mattered for an immature contract and stopped mattering for a mature one — a distinction management never drew.
S5 MEANS: The durable asset is contract maturity and premium quality, not a weekday, so the competitive threat to watch is a rival building longer-dated liquidity rather than one copying an expiry calendar.
BASE-RATE: Realised at this company. Bankex lost 95% of its volume when its calendar advantage went `[Q3FY25 Concall 12Feb2025, MD]` — so calendar risk has cost real money here once, which is precisely why the Sensex counter-example matters: the same company, the same lever, opposite outcomes, distinguished only by product maturity.
S6 IMPLICATION: The earnings stream is more durable than the management narrative implied, because it rests on a mix shift the regulator entrenched rather than on a calendar slot a competitor can take; the monitorable is the share of volume outside the current week, at 5-6% as at May-2026 against management's own admission that "that is not the destination where we would like to be" `[Q4FY26 Concall 13May2026, MD]`, with the next check the Q3FY27 call, and the thesis-breaker is that share stalling below 10% while premium per lot rolls over.
SEVERITY: Medium

### FINDING: The cash-market share ambition would be won in the low-yield leg while the high-yield leg is shrinking twice as fast as volume
S1 WHAT: BSE's cash-segment transaction income fell 30.0% in H1FY26 while its cash turnover fell only 19.3%. The entire excess decline sits in the "special rate" leg — exclusively-listed scrips — which fell 40.1% while the "normal rate" leg tracked volume almost exactly.
S2 WHY: BSE charges a higher rate on scrips listed only on BSE than on scrips traded on both venues `[Q4FY26 Concall 13May2026, MD]`. Cash-market share gains from the common contract note and smart order routing accrue in commonly-traded large caps, because that is what institutions route and what BSE itself says new investors migrate toward. So the mechanism that grows share is structurally the mechanism that dilutes realisation — the share the exchange can win is priced below the share it already has.
S3 EVIDENCE: H1FY26 cash transaction income ₹1,216mn vs H1FY25 ₹1,738mn, −30.0%; cash ADTV ₹75,837mn vs ₹93,963mn, −19.3% `[IP H1FY26 11Nov2025, p13]`. The counter-proportion inside that: normal rate ₹684mn vs ₹850mn = −19.5%, matching the −19.3% volume decline almost exactly, while special rate ₹532mn vs ₹888mn = −40.1%, twice as fast. Special-rate scrips were 888 ÷ 1,738 = 51.1% of H1FY25 cash income and 532 ÷ 1,216 = 43.8% of H1FY26 — the majority of the cash revenue line is the leg that is halving. Realised yield per unit of turnover fell 1,738 ÷ 93,963 = 0.0185 to 1,216 ÷ 75,837 = 0.0160, −13.4%.
S4 CONNECTS: Business — the exclusive-scrip franchise is the SME and smaller-company listing base, which is also BSE's fastest-growing listing engine (700 SMEs, ₹14,735cr raised `[Q3FY26 Concall 13Feb2026, MD]`), so the two halves of the cash story move in opposite directions. Management commentary — the MD's explanation for the special-rate decline is a narrative about investor migration, not a number: "they are more migrating towards the commonly traded large cap or slot of mid-cap stocks. This is how we understand it" `[Q4FY26 Concall 13May2026, MD]`. Income Statement — cash is now a minor line (₹3,017mn FY25 against consolidated transaction charges ₹20,299mn `[IP H1FY26 11Nov2025, p26]`, 14.9%), which is why the yield erosion has gone unremarked. Industry — SEBI's common contract note and closing auction session, both BSE-advocated, are precisely the reforms that make venues fungible in the common-scrip leg. The compensating control is BSE's rising listing base replenishing exclusive scrips; the class of error it cannot catch is migration of *existing* exclusive scrips to dual listing, which produces the same yield loss with no offsetting listing revenue.
DOMAINS: Business, MgmtCommentary, IncomeStatement, Industry
DISCONFIRM: Sought and materially found. Q1FY27 cash ADTV of ₹9,955cr was the highest ever `[Q1FY27 Concall 10Aug2026, MD]`, up 38.7% on Q1FY26's ₹7,180cr, so the volume decline reversed sharply after the period measured; and the split of that quarter's income between rate legs is not published, so the yield trend may have reversed with it. The share of turnover attributable to exclusive scrips is also not disclosed, so the 51.1% revenue share cannot be paired with its volume share -> BSE monthly turnover data by scrip group on bseindia.com, and the FY26 annual report segment note.
S5 MEANS: Growth in cash market share converts to revenue at a materially worse rate than BSE's historical cash realisation, so the double-digit-share ambition is worth less per point of share than the current mix implies.
BASE-RATE: Realised, not theoretical — ₹522mn (1,738 − 1,216) of actual cash transaction income was lost year-on-year in H1FY26, of which ₹356mn (888 − 532) came from the special-rate leg alone, 68.2% of the total shortfall from 51.1% of the base.
S6 IMPLICATION: Cash-segment revenue should be modelled on the two rate legs separately rather than on blended ADTV, because a share-driven volume recovery in common scrips lifts turnover faster than it lifts revenue; the monitorable is the special-rate share of cash transaction income, 43.8% at H1FY26, next check the H1FY27 investor presentation, and the thesis-breaker is that leg falling below roughly a third of cash income while the double-digit-share target is still being quoted.
SEVERITY: Medium

### FINDING: Five metrics were retired at or just after the moment they turned unflattering, and the only market share BSE still prints is the one at 90%
S1 WHAT: Derivatives market share, Bankex volumes, the ₹170cr SEBI regulatory-fee dispute and StAR MF 2.0 were each disclosed and then stopped being disclosed; treasury income on clearing and settlement funds left the spoken narrative while remaining in the deck. In every case the disclosure or the narration ceased at or immediately after the point at which the underlying number turned adverse, and in no case was the cessation explained. Re-tested against the Feb-2026, May-2026 and Aug-2026 decks: none of the withdrawn series has returned, and the single surviving market-share figure has since gone too.
S2 WHY: Nothing compels a quarterly narrative to be complete, and the prepared statement is the one disclosure channel management controls entirely — unlike the financial results, which are audited, or the presentation, which carries comparatives. Dropping a line from the speech removes it from the public conversation without triggering any restatement obligation. That is why the pattern shows up in the speech first: treasury income on C&S funds still appears in the deck's P&L table while vanishing from three consecutive prepared statements.
S3 EVIDENCE: Across seven consecutive investor presentations covering 21 months — `[IP H1FY25 12Nov2024]` `[IP Q3FY25 06Feb2025]` `[IP Q1FY26 07Aug2025]` `[IP H1FY26 11Nov2025]` `[Q3FY26 Deck 09Feb2026]` `[Q4FY26 Deck 07May2026]` `[Q1FY27 Deck 04Aug2026]` — exactly one market-share figure is printed anywhere, in exactly one deck: "Commanding a 90% market share in the mutual fund segment" `[IP Q1FY26 07Aug2025, p18]`. The counter-proportion is that zero share figures are printed for equity cash, equity derivatives or clearing, the three contested segments; 1 of 1 published share metrics was the one at 90%, 0 of 3 contested ones ever appeared, and the 90% line itself is gone from the Q3FY26 ICCL slide `[Q3FY26 Deck 09Feb2026, PDF p18]`. On Bankex: after the 95% collapse was disclosed `[Q3FY25 Concall 12Feb2025, MD]` together with "we are confident we will rebuild the monthly contract of Bankex", the word appears zero times in the Q4FY25, Q1FY26, Q2FY26 and Q1FY27 concalls, numberless once each in Q3FY26 and Q4FY26, and in the three 2026 decks only as an expiry-date footnote — 18 months, six calls and three decks without a figure `[Q3FY26 Deck 09Feb2026, PDF p14]`. On the ₹170cr fee: two mentions, then absent from twelve documents over 24 months.
S4 CONNECTS: Governance — the disclosure set narrows precisely where accountability would bite, and the seven offline routings show the same valve operating live (§c3). Management commentary — the removal of the derivatives share slide was noticed by an analyst and answered with a doctrine ("we do not measure the market share at BSE in a normal way" `[Q4FY24 Concall 13May2024, MD]`) that management then abandoned for cash, where it set an explicit double-digit target `[Q1FY27 Concall 10Aug2026, MD]`. Income Statement — the FY24 comparative for Regulatory Contribution moved 3,286 to 2,369, exactly the ₹917mn core SGF `[IP H1FY25 12Nov2024, p25]` `[IP Q3FY25 06Feb2025, p24]`, with only boilerplate covering it. Business — the one product management promised to rebuild is the one it stopped numbering, so the rebuild claim is unfalsifiable by construction. The compensating control is that the audited results still carry every line; the class of error it cannot catch is deterioration inside an aggregate that was never disaggregated in the first place, such as Bankex inside total equity-derivative premium.
DOMAINS: Governance, MgmtCommentary, IncomeStatement, Business
DISCONFIRM: Sought and materially found, and one limb of the original finding does not survive. **Treasury income on C&S funds was never withdrawn from disclosure** — it remains a printed line in all three 2026 decks, and the FY26 deck even adds an explicit −21% column against FY25, ₹1,714mn versus ₹2,178mn `[Q4FY26 Deck 07May2026, PDF p26]` `[Q1FY27 Deck 04Aug2026, PDF p26]`. It left the MD's spoken remarks only, which is a narration choice, not a disclosure withdrawal, and it is reclassified to §c2 accordingly. Colocation revenue moved the opposite way — refused in Aug-2025 ("We are not publishing it separately" `[Q1FY26 Concall 13Aug2025, CFO]`) then disclosed quarterly from Nov-2025 and retrospectively for FY25 `[Q4FY26 Concall 13May2026, MD]` — so the disclosure set is not monotonically narrowing. The derivatives share slide also has a defensible rationale: BSE's own data show notional-based share is a poor economic proxy, since premium per lot rose 180% while lots fell 35% (see the preceding finding). And the FY24 reclassification is covered by the deck's standing "regrouped/reclassified wherever necessary" disclaimer. What survives disconfirmation is narrower than the first pass claimed but still holds: on the four series that genuinely left the public record, removal clustered on adverse turns, and none has returned in the eighteen months since.
S5 MEANS: The public numbers are a curated subset chosen after the outcome is known, so an absent series should be read as evidence about the series rather than as an absence of evidence.
BASE-RATE: Realised at this company. The one metric whose post-removal path can be reconstructed is Bankex, which fell 95% `[Q3FY25 Concall 12Feb2025, MD]`; and the item dropped from the narrative in Aug-2024 was a live ₹170cr provision that had already been charged against FY24 profit `[Q4FY24 Concall 13May2024, MD]`, reducing the FY24 dividend payout ratio to 71.3% from 99% the prior year `[IP H1FY26 11Nov2025, p31]`. Disclosure withdrawal here has twice preceded or accompanied a real economic loss.
S6 IMPLICATION: Any line item that has left the prepared remarks should be treated as deteriorating until the audited statements prove otherwise, and the quality of the earnings stream should be discounted for the fact that its most volatile components are the least narrated; the monitorable is whether Bankex, treasury income on C&S funds and the SEBI fee dispute reappear with figures in the FY27 annual report, and the thesis-breaker is a sixth metric leaving the narrative in the quarter it turns.
SEVERITY: High

### FINDING: BSE has one prior new-product launch it declared a success in month one, and the run-rate decayed 95% within six months — the base rate that governs Focused IT
S1 WHAT: Single stock derivatives launched 1-Jul-2024 and were declared to have "exceeded our expectations" after one month. The futures run-rate then fell from ₹316cr in month 1 to ₹16cr per month by months 5-7, and management's description reversed to "yet to pick up volumes in a meaningful way." The identical month-one framing is now being applied to the Focused IT Index derivatives launched 11-May-2026.
S2 WHY: A first month of a new derivative captures curiosity flow and member testing, not a liquidity pool. The binding constraint is broker technology adoption, which management identified but could not schedule: "there are contending technological changes at every broker's end… how they will be able to prioritize is something which they will be only able to provide, and it will not be a common single date" `[Q2FY25 Concall 19Nov2024, MD]`. Declaring success on month-one flow therefore measures the launch, not the product.
S3 EVIDENCE: Month 1 (Jul-2024) turnover ₹316cr futures and ₹35cr options `[Q1FY25 Concall 13Aug2024, MD]`; since-relaunch cumulative ₹516cr futures at Nov-2024 `[Q2FY25 Concall 19Nov2024, MD]` and ₹564cr at Feb-2025 `[Q3FY25 Concall 12Feb2025, MD]`. Months 2-4 therefore added 516 − 316 = ₹200cr, ₹66.7cr per month; months 5-7 added 564 − 516 = ₹48cr, ₹16cr per month — a 94.9% decay from the month-one rate. The counter-proportion is the other leg going the opposite way: futures were 316 ÷ 35 = 9.0x options in month 1, and by FY25-end cumulative futures ₹830cr against options ₹2,773cr = 0.30x `[Q4FY25 Concall 09May2025, MD]`. The leg management pointed to when declaring success is the leg that died, and the leg it barely mentioned is the one that grew 79-fold.
S4 CONNECTS: Business — three years on, stock options remain unbuilt and management now concedes the difficulty explicitly: "how to bring in liquidity in a product where there is no product differentiation in itself is a challenge… it's aspirational" `[Q1FY27 Concall 10Aug2026, MD]`. Management commentary — the same month-one framing is live again: Focused IT has "already completed three expiry cycles, with trading activity and market participation continuing to gain momentum every passing expiry cycle", with no figure `[Q1FY27 Concall 10Aug2026, MD]`. Income Statement — single stock derivatives never became a disclosed revenue line and the equity-derivative revenue series is presented as a single number `[IP H1FY26 11Nov2025, p14]`, so the failure was absorbed invisibly. Industry — the same regulator-driven lot-size and expiry rules that helped Sensex did nothing for a product with no differentiation. The compensating control is that failed launches at an exchange cost little incremental capital; the class of error it cannot catch is management credibility spent on qualitative success claims that later reverse.
DOMAINS: Business, MgmtCommentary, IncomeStatement, Industry
DISCONFIRM: Sought and found on the other side. BSE's launch execution is genuinely reliable — every announced launch date in the window was met: single stock derivatives 1-Jul-2024, India INX Sensex 3-Feb-2025, Focused IT 11-May-2026, StAR NPS 22-Apr-2026 `[Q4FY26 Concall 13May2026, MD]` `[Q1FY27 Concall 10Aug2026, MD]`. Focused IT also differs from single stock derivatives in the way that matters — it is a single index contract with a defined hedging use case rather than 200-plus undifferentiated underlyings, which is precisely the distinction management itself draws. And Sensex options, the one product that did work, took roughly 30 months to mature, so three expiry cycles is genuinely too early to judge.
S5 MEANS: Management's qualitative verdict on a product's early traction has no predictive content, and the only new-product claim worth acting on is one carrying a turnover figure.
BASE-RATE: Realised at this company, once, through this exact channel. The last product declared a month-one success decayed 94.9% in run-rate within six months and was reclassified by management itself as not having picked up meaningfully. That is a sample of one, which is why the finding is a prior rather than a prediction.
S6 IMPLICATION: No value should be carried for Focused IT, Focused MidCap, Sensex Next 30, commodities or electricity derivatives until a turnover number is published, and the driver assumptions should treat the derivative franchise as one product plus optionality rather than a pipeline; the monitorable is whether the Q2FY27 investor presentation prints Focused IT turnover separately, and the thesis-breaker is a second successive launch narrated qualitatively for three or more quarters without a figure.
SEVERITY: Medium

### FINDING: The payout ratio has fallen from 99% to 16.5% in three years and both times it was questioned management answered with a different metric
S1 WHAT: BSE's dividend payout ratio went 99% (FY23) to 71% (FY24) to 28% (FY25) to 17.8% (FY26). Asked about this directly in two successive annual calls, management answered with absolute dividend rupees the first time and with market capitalisation the second, and never addressed the ratio itself.
S2 WHY: The stated use of the retained cash is real — technology and data-centre capex, plus a deliberately over-capitalised balance sheet to win clearing members: "a stronger balance sheet of an exchange is very essential to showcase to the world that the clearing corporation is strong enough" `[Q4FY26 Concall 13May2026, MD]`. That is a genuine strategic reason, and it worked, since ICCL's clearing share reached 25-27% `[Q3FY26 Concall 13Feb2026]`. What is missing is not a reason but a policy: there is no stated payout framework, no target ratio and no capital-allocation disclosure, so the ratio is a residual of undisclosed decisions rather than an outcome of a rule.
S3 EVIDENCE: Payout ratio 99% (2022-23), 71% (2023-24), 28% (2024-25), computed by BSE on standalone profit `[IP H1FY26 11Nov2025, p31]`; the FY25 figure ties out as ₹316cr ÷ ₹11,125mn standalone PAT = 28.4%, exactly as management stated `[Q4FY25 Concall 09May2025, MD]` `[IP H1FY26 11Nov2025, p28]`. FY26 is now `DERIVED` from the audited figure rather than estimated: standalone net profit excluding the exceptional item was ₹23,183mn `[Q4FY26 Deck 07May2026, PDF p29]`, so 412 ÷ 2,318.3 = **17.8%** — the ratio has fallen **81.2 percentage points** in three years (99.0 − 17.8). *(My first pass estimated 19.7% by scaling consolidated PAT at the FY25 standalone-to-consolidated ratio of 0.839; the audited figure is 1.9pp lower, so the estimate was conservative in management's favour.)* The counter-proportion is the metric management substitutes, and it is genuinely flattering: absolute dividend ₹154cr, ₹204cr, ₹316cr, ₹412cr — 412 ÷ 154 = 2.67x, +167.5% over three years `[Q4FY25 Concall 09May2025, MD]` `[Q4FY26 Concall 13May2026, MD]`. Both series are true at once; the ratio collapsed while the rupees nearly tripled, because consolidated profit grew 24,970 ÷ 2,057 = 12.1x over the same window `[IP H1FY26 11Nov2025, p30]` `[Q4FY26 Concall 13May2026, MD]`. Management quotes only the second series.
S4 CONNECTS: Governance — the substitution escalated between the two calls, from absolute rupees to share-price appreciation ("the market capitalization of BSE was 5,000 crores when I joined. And today we are talking about a market capitalization of around 1.56 lakh crores" `[Q4FY26 Concall 13May2026, MD]`), which is not a distribution at all. Cash Flow — H1FY26 investing outflow of ₹313cr was the largest recent capex block and its composition was refused `[Q2FY26 Concall 17Nov2025, MD]`, so the retained cash cannot be traced to specific assets from public disclosure. Balance Sheet — consolidated net worth rose ₹33,023mn (FY24) to ₹52,108mn (H1FY26), +57.8% `[IP H1FY26 11Nov2025, p31]`, confirming the retention is real and is accumulating. Management commentary — the FY27 technology budget was flagged as "almost going to be doubling" from ₹300cr `[Q4FY26 Concall 13May2026, MD]`, which is the first forward-looking use of the retained cash management has quantified. The compensating control is that no dilution occurred and the buyback take-up claim checks out; the class of error it cannot catch is capital retained at group level and deployed into subsidiaries whose returns are not separately reported.
DOMAINS: Governance, MgmtCommentary, CashFlow, BalanceSheet
DISCONFIRM: Sought and substantially found, and the new audited evidence strengthens management's side. **The retained cash was demonstrably deployed, not accumulated idly**: FY26 purchases of property, plant and equipment, intangibles, capital work in progress and capital advances were ₹51,516 lakhs against ₹15,323 lakhs in FY25, a 3.4x step-up `[Q4FY26 Results 07May2026, PDF p8]` — ₹515cr of capex against ₹412cr of dividend, so investment exceeded distribution in the year the payout ratio fell to 17.8%. And it was capitalised rather than parked: PPE rose ₹22,217 lakhs to ₹56,259 lakhs while CWIP *fell* ₹4,073 lakhs to ₹3,255 lakhs `[Q4FY26 Results 07May2026, PDF p7]`. There has been no equity dilution — 13,53,76,359 shares times 3 for the 1:2 bonus equals 40,61,29,077 exactly `[IP Q3FY25 06Feb2025, p30]` `[IP Q1FY26 07Aug2025, p32]`. The buyback anecdote is corroborated by BSE's own table, FY24 buyback ₹93mn against FY20's ₹4,762mn, 2.0% take-up `[IP H1FY26 11Nov2025, p31]`. The FY26 dividend arithmetic management quoted is correct on both bases (+30.4% and +66.7%). The retained cash is not idle: investment income is disclosed and material, and the MD explicitly rejected the 4-4.5% return premise. And a 99% payout at FY23 profit levels was itself a signal of no reinvestment opportunity, which management named directly — "A 100% dividend payout… may indicate a situation where the company is not having any growth idea at all" `[Q4FY26 Concall 13May2026, MD]`. That is a legitimate answer; the objection is that it was offered only after two evasions.
S5 MEANS: Capital allocation at BSE is discretionary and undocumented rather than policy-driven, so the retention rate is a management variable an outside investor cannot forecast or hold to a standard.
BASE-RATE: No realised loss found. Retained capital has so far been accompanied by rising profits, a 2.31x increase in colocation revenue and a clearing share gain, and no impairment or write-off of a retained-capital deployment appears in any transcript or deck in the window. The single ECL provision that did appear, ₹40cr against an NSE receivable, is a counterparty matter rather than a capital-deployment failure. This is a governance and predictability finding, not yet an economic one.
S6 IMPLICATION: Distributable cash cannot be modelled from history because the payout ratio is a residual of undisclosed capex decisions, so the driver assumptions should carry retention as a wide range rather than a trend; the monitorable is whether the FY27 dividend keeps the ratio below 20% while the technology budget doubles as flagged, next check the Q4FY27 call, and the thesis-breaker is a further ratio decline accompanied by a third successive refusal to publish the capex composition.
SEVERITY: Medium

## Method note

Verdicts are assigned only where the evidence set settles the outcome. Where it does not, the item is
marked `Too early to tell` or `NOT DISCLOSED` and the settling document is named — never guessed.
Derived figures show their arithmetic inline and every derived value carrying a conclusion was
recomputed independently.

**Source priority as applied here.** The second pass added limited-reviewed and audited financial
results, which outrank both the concall and the deck. Where they were available they were used as the
source of record: the Q3FY26 EBITDA reconstruction and the B7 standalone ratio are tied to
`[Q3FY26 Results 09Feb2026]`, and the FY26 capex and balance-sheet figures to
`[Q4FY26 Results 07May2026]`. Decks are used to establish what management claimed and, where a series
appears nowhere else, as the comparative record management itself published; per the source-priority
rule they are never the source of an audited aggregate. The FY26 annual report supersedes any figure
here that it contradicts.

**What the second pass changed.** One verdict (B7) and one derived figure (the FY26 payout ratio,
estimated at 19.7%, audited at 17.8%). Three observations were weakened or corrected: treasury income on
C&S funds is a narration withdrawal rather than a disclosure withdrawal; the capex/CWIP hypothesis in
§c2 is falsified; and the J8 margin discrepancy is a spoken error the same-day deck got right. Two were
strengthened: Bankex has now been un-numbered for 18 months across six calls and three decks, and the
one market-share figure BSE ever printed has itself been withdrawn. Nothing was deleted — per the
contradiction-adjudication rule, every correction is shown against the position it replaced.
