# W3 — Group structure & related-party ledger — BSE Limited (FY2017–FY2026)

**Wave 3 · owner: group & RPT · date 2026-09-08 · source of record: the annual-report PDFs at
`data/text/BSE-AR-FY*.txt`.** Page tags are `PDF pN / printed prN`; the offset is per document from
`records/printed_page_offsets.json` (FY26 and FY25 +11, FY24 +6, FY22 +9, FY21 +5, FY20 +7, FY19 +9,
FY18 +7, FY17 +2) and every tag below was checked against the running head on the page itself.

**The frame this wave works in.** BSE has **no promoter** — the consolidated accounts say so in terms:
*"There are no promoters identified for the Company"* `[AR FY26, Consolidated Note 52(i), PDF p240/pr229]`.
The ordinary promoter-RPT question therefore has no subject, and W1.7 §4 is right that recording those
tests as passes would be a false clean. What replaces them is **intra-group flow** — the group prices
services to itself — and **the conflict that BSE regulates entities it also earns from**. Both are
below, sized.

**The one-line answer to the wave's central question.** The group's related-party ledger is small in
rupees and almost entirely intra-group; its problems are not theft, they are **disclosure**. Four
numbers in the FY2026 annual report cannot be reconciled to other numbers in the same annual report,
and each of the four sits on a related-party line.

### 1.2 The two structural events across the decade

**(a) The CDSL deconsolidation, FY2018.** CDSL went from a 50.05% consolidated subsidiary
`[AR FY17, Form AOC-1, PDF p268/pr266]` to an equity-accounted associate, and BSE has been a seller ever
since: **20%** at 31-Mar-2022 `[AR FY22, Form AOC-1 Part B, PDF p298/pr289]` and **15%** at 31-Mar-2024
`[AR FY24, Form AOC-1 Part B, PDF p313/pr307]`. The two sell-downs produced the two largest exceptional
gains in BSE's history: **₹9,158 lakh** standalone on the FY2020 4% offer-for-sale
`[AR FY20, MD&A, PDF p116/pr109]` and **₹50,417 lakh** standalone / **₹40,662 lakh**
consolidated on the FY2024 5% disposal `[AR FY24, Standalone and Consolidated P&L, PDF p247/pr241 and
PDF p157/pr151]`. W1_docmap is right that the two-segment consolidated disclosure stopped after FY2017 —
it stopped because the depository segment left the consolidation, not because the disclosure was
withdrawn. That is the benign reading and it holds.

**(b) The FY2026 pruning.** Five entities left the group in a single day, 1–2 May 2025
`[AR FY26, Board's Report, PDF p28/pr17]`:

| Entity | What happened | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| BSE Institute Ltd | **SOLD**, 2-May-2025 | Training |
| BSE Institute of Research Development & Innovation | **SOLD** (BIL's s.8 subsidiary) | Training |
| BFSI Sector Skill Council of India | ceased subsidiary → associate 48.78% | Training |
| BSE Investments Ltd | merged into BTPL, appointed date 1-Apr-2025 | Investment holding |
| BSE Administration & Supervision Ltd (BASL) | merged into BTPL, appointed date 1-Apr-2025 | Supervision of SEBI-registered advisers |
| EBIX Insuretech Pvt Ltd | associate divested by BTPL, 9-Dec-2025 | Insurtech |

The merger of BSE Investments and BASL into BTPL ties arithmetically: BSE's standalone carrying value of
BTPL rose 4,250 → 12,650, and **DERIVED 4,250 + 8,300 (BSE Investments) + 100 (BASL) = 12,650**
`[AR FY26, Standalone Note 7, PDF p267/pr256]`.

**The three entities sold or declassified were all "training" entities.** SEBI penalised BSE ₹3 lakh by
Order of 29-Jul-2022 for *"engaging in unrelated / non-incidental activities by BSE or its
subsidiaries"* under Reg 38(2) of the SECC Regulations 2018; SAT set the order aside on 2-May-2025
`[AR FY26, CG Report §8B item 2, PDF p94/pr83]`. The AR nowhere links the two, and this wave does not
assert the link — but the pruning date (1–2 May 2025) and the SAT order date (2-May-2025) coincide, and
the activities pruned are the ones SEBI named.

### 1.3 Which subsidiary was sold, and to whom

**BSE Institute Limited (BIL), a 100% subsidiary, sold on 2 May 2025.** In-principle board approval
8-May-2024; SPA signed 2-May-2025; classified held-for-sale in FY2025 and reported as a discontinued
operation `[AR FY26, Consolidated Note 49, PDF p238/pr227]`.

**The buyer is named nowhere in the annual report.** It is named in one place in the whole corpus — the
Q4FY25 analyst presentation: *"BSE has executed a Share Purchase Agreement ('SPA') on May 2, 2025, to
divest its 100% stake in BSE Institute Limited (BIL) to **AV Financial Experts Network Private
Limited**"* `[IP 06-May-2025, BSE Analysts & Institutional Investors presentation, PDF p9]`. Per doctrine
§5 a presentation establishes what management claimed and cannot carry an L1; here it carries the only
disclosure of a counterparty identity that the audited statements omit entirely.

**And the two statements disagree on what BSE was paid.** See §5.3 and CHAIN: Divestment of BSE
Institute Limited.

**DERIVED: 2,33,416 + 3,517 + 6,542 + 973 + 5,250 = 2,49,698.** The subsidiary sum is
**DERIVED 6,805 − 3,450 + 4 − 2,615 + 522 + 2,268 − 11 − 6 = 3,517** and the associate sum is

`[AR FY26, Consolidated Note 40, PDF p229/pr218; Consolidated P&L, PDF p164/pr153]`.

consolidation. If that elimination is charged to the parent's line, the gross unexplained upward
adjustment is **DERIVED 5,250 + 6,118 = 11,368**. Nothing in the annual report reconciles it.

### 3.3 The arm's-length assertion, and what it is worth

Both notes carry the identical boilerplate: *"The sales to and purchases from related parties are made
on terms equivalent to those that prevail in arm's length transactions. Outstanding balances at the
year-end are unsecured and interest free and settlement occurs in cash. There have been no guarantees
provided or received for any related party receivables or payables."*
`[AR FY26, Standalone Note 35, PDF p302/pr291; Consolidated Note 36, PDF p227/pr216]`

**AOC-2 is declared not applicable** — *"none of the transactions with related parties fell under Section
188(1) of the Act"* `[AR FY26, Board's Report §D, PDF p28/pr17]`, repeated verbatim in the CG report
`[AR FY26, CG Report §8A, PDF p94/pr83]`. **W1_docmap flagged this as an assertion to test. It tests
clean on the arithmetic.** The Reg 23 materiality threshold is the lower of ₹1,000 cr and 10% of
consolidated annual turnover; 10% of consolidated total income is **DERIVED 5,14,810 × 10% = 51,481**
`[AR FY26, Consolidated P&L, PDF p164/pr153]`. The largest single counterparty aggregate is ICCL at
**DERIVED 5,317 (income) + 12,619 (expenditure) + 31 (liability transfers) = 17,967**, i.e. **34.9% of
the threshold**. No shareholder approval was required, and the assertion is arithmetically sound.

**What is not sound is the pricing basis.** Not one of the recurring intra-group charges — clearing house
expenses, software usage charges, rent and infrastructure charges, technology expense, index management
fees, royalty — carries a rate, a volume base, a per-unit price, or a benchmark anywhere in the corpus.
`NOT-DISCLOSED -> the RPT Policy Framework hosted at bseindia.com/investor_relations/corporategovernance.html
(referred to but not reproduced in the AR) and the audit committee's omnibus approval record.`
The assertion of arm's length is therefore **unfalsifiable from the filings**, which is the finding.

### 4.3 RF-025 confirmed on the arithmetic

- Standalone fall: **DERIVED 38,967 − 37,191 = 1,776, i.e. −4.56%**
- Elimination change: **DERIVED 12,607 − 7,111 = 5,496, i.e. +77.29%**
- **DERIVED 5,496 / 6,770 = 81.18%** of the consolidated fall is the intra-group charge going **up** and
  netting out. RF-025's numbers are correct in every particular.

### 4.4 But the mechanism is not what RF-025 assumed, and that makes it a better finding

RF-025 read the movement as an accounting artefact concealing a rising cost. **It is not. It is a real
in-sourcing, and the disclosure is what fails.** The counter-proportion RF-025 did not carry:

- **BSE's external clearing cost fell 22.8%** (**DERIVED 31,856 → 24,584 = −7,272, −22.83%**) while the
  related-party portion rose 77.3%. The related-party share of BSE's clearing spend went
  **DERIVED 7,111 / 38,967 = 18.25% → 12,607 / 37,191 = 33.90%**.
- **Per unit of activity the whole line halved.** Standalone transaction charges are 1,94,987 (FY25) and
  3,66,948 (FY26) `[AR FY26, Standalone Note 22, PDF p285/pr274]`. Total clearing cost per rupee of
  transaction charge: **DERIVED 38,967 / 1,94,987 = 19.98% → 37,191 / 3,66,948 = 10.14%**. External:
  **DERIVED 31,856 / 1,94,987 = 16.34% → 24,584 / 3,66,948 = 6.70%**. Related-party:
  **DERIVED 7,111 / 1,94,987 = 3.65% → 12,607 / 3,66,948 = 3.44%**.

**So the ICCL charge got cheaper per unit of trade, not dearer.** On the only arm's-length proxy the
filings permit, the charge passes.

**The mechanism, in the MD's own words.** Asked on 17-Nov-2025 what drove the group's clearing share, the
MD & CEO said ICCL's per-broker clearing capacity had gone from 50 trades per second to about 27,000,
that *"this has led to some of the major brokers considering ICCL as their clearing corporation and that
shift has taken us to the higher market share"*, and that *"the clearing charges income of ICCL is around
Rs. 32 crores this quarter"* `[Q2FY26 Concall 17-Nov-2025, MD & CEO]`. Annualised that is ~12,800, which
sits alongside the **ESTIMATE 42,464 − 12,607 − 17,142 = 12,715** of ICCL's non-parent, non-treasury
revenue `[AR FY26, Form AOC-1 PDF p312/pr301; Standalone Note 35 PDF p298/pr287; Consolidated Note 22
PDF p211/pr200]` (assumption: all of the group's ₹17,142 lakh treasury income from clearing and
settlement funds arises in ICCL; India ICC's entire revenue is 947, so the error is bounded by ~1,000).

**That is the whole story, and the MD&A tells none of it.** Under SEBI's interoperability framework a
trading member chooses its clearing corporation, so BSE's clearing bill splits between its own ICCL and
the interoperating clearing corporation. In FY2026 members moved clearing onto ICCL; BSE paid its rival
less and its subsidiary more; the group's total external clearing cost fell 19.4%. The MD&A instead
writes: *"Clearing and settlement expenses — paid to clearing corporations — declined 5% to ₹37,191 Lakh
despite significantly higher transaction turnover reflecting increasing depth Index derivative contract
is gaining, this represents a sustainable structural efficiency gain for the Company"*
`[AR FY26, MD&A, PDF p76/pr65]`. Three things are wrong with that sentence: it attributes the movement to
product depth rather than to a clearing-venue shift; it calls "sustainable" a share gain a competitor can
reverse; and it describes a line one-third of which is a related-party charge without saying so.

### 4.5 Where the money actually went — the consequence nobody states

The charge is EPS-neutral at group level because ICCL is wholly owned. It is **not** neutral for
distributable profit. **₹12,607 lakh of pre-tax profit moved out of BSE Limited — the only entity that
can pay a dividend — and into a SEBI-regulated clearing corporation with its own net-worth floor and its
own Core SGF obligations.** ICCL returned **3,540** of it as dividend, the first ICCL dividend since
FY2024. **DERIVED 12,607 − 3,540 = 9,067 lakh retained inside ICCL in FY2026 alone.** On the same call
where the MD explained the market-share shift, he described ICCL's dividend as set partly to preserve
*"confidence to people who want to use our clearing and settlement system"* `[Q4FY25 Concall 09-May-2025,
MD & CEO]` — i.e. ICCL's retention is a regulatory-capital decision, not a shareholder-return decision.
This is the mechanical half of RF-010's payout collapse and it belongs in W4_capalloc.

**ICCL's dependence on its parent has doubled.** The parent's charge is **DERIVED 12,607 / 42,464 = 29.7%
of ICCL's turnover** (FY2025: **DERIVED 7,111 / 39,925 = 17.8%**) and **DERIVED 12,607 / 22,867 = 55.1%
of ICCL's PBT** (FY2025: **DERIVED 7,111 / 21,538 = 33.0%**) `[AR FY26, Form AOC-1, PDF p312/pr301;
AR FY25, Form AOC-1, PDF p278/pr267]`. **ICCL's revenue from everyone other than its own parent fell:
DERIVED 39,925 − 7,111 = 32,814 → 42,464 − 12,607 = 29,857, i.e. −9.0%.** Its total turnover rose 6.4%
only because the price it charged its parent rose 77.3%.

(DERIVED 3,919 / 3,10,368 = 1.26% of reported CFO)**, and the same happened in FY2025 with 3,449 —
**DERIVED 3,919 + 3,449 = 7,368 over two years**. RF-028 upgrades from Watch to a two-year classification
defect and routes to W4_cash, where it compounds the float problem of RF-005.

## 6. THE STRUCTURAL CONFLICT — BSE EARNS FROM WHAT IT SUPERVISES

### 6.1 Listing: the conflict W1.7 §4.3 named, sized

Standalone listing services revenue is **51,893** — listing fees 38,861, book building software charges
10,412, other fees 2,620 `[AR FY26, Standalone Note 22, PDF p285/pr274]` — **DERIVED 51,893 / 4,46,951 =
11.61% of standalone revenue from operations**, paid by the issuers BSE supervises as an exchange. This is
structural, not a scandal, and it is stated here because W1.7 requires it be stated rather than assumed
away. **No listed issuer appears anywhere in either RPT note**, so no listing relationship is a related
party. The only listed entity inside the group perimeter is CDSL.

### 6.2 The supervision of advisers moved *into* the fee-earning parent

**This is the change in the year and it runs the wrong way.**

Until FY2025 the supervisory function sat in a separate subsidiary: **BSE Administration & Supervision
Limited, principal activity "Administration and supervision of SEBI Registered Advisers"**
`[AR FY26, Consolidated Note 40.1, PDF p171/pr160]`. Then SEBI, by circular of 12-Jul-2024, granted
**BSE Limited itself** recognition as **Research Analyst Administration and Supervisory Body (RAASB)** and
**Investment Adviser Administration and Supervisory Body (IAASB)** for five years from 25-Jul-2024
`[AR FY26, MD&A, PDF p65/pr54]`. In FY2026 BSE inspected **84 Investment Advisers and 71 Research
Analysts** in that capacity `[AR FY26, MD&A, PDF p64/pr53]`.

The RPT note records the handover: **BASL transferred ₹529 lakh of "annual membership and subscription
fees" to BSE Limited in FY2025** `[AR FY26, Standalone Note 35, PDF p297/pr286]`. BASL was then merged
into **BSE Technologies Private Limited — the group's IT-services company** — with appointed date
1-Apr-2025 `[AR FY26, Board's Report, PDF p28/pr17]`.

**So the regulator's fee income and the regulated relationship both now sit inside BSE Limited, whose
consolidated profit is 95.6% of the group's, and the residual corporate shell was folded into a company
that sells technology to market participants.** The adviser-supervision fee stream is **not separately
disclosed**: it is inside "Annual subscription and processing fees 4,448" or "Other fees 2,620"
`[AR FY26, Standalone Note 22, PDF p285/pr274]`. `NOT-DISCLOSED -> the RAASB/IAASB fee schedule filed
with SEBI under the 12-Jul-2024 circular, and BSE's segment note, which reports one segment only.`

### 6.4 Who audits what

ICCL is the group's only material subsidiary and its statutory auditor is **KKC & Associates LLP**, not
the group auditor `[AR FY26, CG Report §8O, PDF p96/pr85]`. The consolidated audit report's Other Matter
reads: *"We did not audit the financial statements … in respect of six subsidiaries, whose Ind AS
financial statements include total assets of Rs 6,24,532 lakhs as at March 31, 2026, and total revenues
of Rs 38,458 lakhs"* `[AR FY26, Consolidated Auditor's Report, PDF p157/pr146]`.

**The asymmetry is the finding: 46.4% of consolidated total assets, 7.5% of consolidated total income.**
**DERIVED 6,24,532 / 13,44,614 = 46.45%** against **DERIVED 38,458 / 5,14,810 = 7.47%**
`[AR FY26, Consolidated Balance Sheet, PDF p163/pr152; Consolidated P&L, PDF p164/pr153]`. Nearly half the
group's balance sheet — the half holding member margin, settlement obligations and the ₹1,23,264 lakh
Core SGF — is audited by someone else, and the consolidated audit report carries **one** key audit matter,
IT systems and controls. The class of error an ITGC KAM cannot catch is precisely the one this structure
exposes: valuation and classification inside the clearing corporation's balance sheet, which is exactly
where the ₹56,751 lakh reserve reclassification of §4.6 occurred.

### 6.5 A rival exchange's money sits in this group's clearing fund

ICCL's Core SGF at 31-Mar-2026 is **1,23,264**, contributed **ICCL 60,896 · BSE 17,952 · NSE 26,529 ·
MSE 934 · other 16,953** `[AR FY26, Consolidated Note 41, PDF p232/pr221]`. A year earlier the same fund
stood at **DERIVED 56,751 + 15,318 + 24,760 + 869 + 14,345 = 1,12,043**, of which NSE's 24,760
`[AR FY25, Consolidated Note 42, PDF p213/pr202]`. **NSE's money is 21.5% of the fund
(DERIVED 26,529 / 1,23,264 = 21.52%, against 24,760 / 1,12,043 = 22.10% a year earlier), and in rupees
it rose DERIVED 26,529 / 24,760 − 1 = +7.1%.** W2_business's observation is confirmed
and it cuts both ways: interoperability means BSE's clearing arm holds its rival's collateral, and BSE
pays its rival's clearing arm whenever a BSE trade clears there. **That is the same mechanism as §4.4,
seen from the balance sheet.**

## 9. FINDINGS

### FINDING: The clearing-cost "structural efficiency" is a related-party in-sourcing the MD&A never names
S1 WHAT: BSE's standalone clearing and settlement expense fell 4.6% to 37,191 and the consolidated line fell 19.4% to 28,035, and the MD&A calls the standalone fall "a sustainable structural efficiency gain for the Company" attributable to "increasing depth Index derivative contract is gaining" `[AR FY26, MD&A, PDF p76/pr65]`. Inside that line, the amount BSE pays its own clearing corporation rose 77.3%, from 7,111 to 12,607 `[AR FY26, Standalone Note 35, PDF p298/pr287]`.
S2 WHY: SEBI's interoperability framework lets a trading member choose its clearing corporation, so BSE's clearing bill splits between ICCL and the interoperating clearing corporation. ICCL raised per-broker clearing capacity from 50 trades per second to about 27,000, and the MD & CEO told the market on 17-Nov-2025 that "this has led to some of the major brokers considering ICCL as their clearing corporation and that shift has taken us to the higher market share" `[Q2FY26 Concall 17-Nov-2025, MD & CEO]`. Clearing moved in-house. The mechanism is a venue shift, not a rate or a product.
S3 EVIDENCE: DERIVED 34,805 − 28,035 = 6,770 consolidated fall; DERIVED 12,607 − 7,111 = 5,496 rise in the eliminated intra-group charge; DERIVED 5,496 / 6,770 = 81.18%. The counter-proportion: BSE's external clearing cost fell DERIVED 31,856 → 24,584 = −7,272, −22.83%, so the related-party share of BSE's clearing spend went DERIVED 7,111 / 38,967 = 18.25% to 12,607 / 37,191 = 33.90%. The elimination ties exactly in every year from FY2019 to FY2026 (§4.2). Per unit of activity the whole line halved: DERIVED 38,967 / 1,94,987 = 19.98% to 37,191 / 3,66,948 = 10.14% of transaction charges, while the related-party rate fell DERIVED 3.65% to 3.44%.
S4 CONNECTS: Income statement (both bases), Notes (the standalone RPT note is the only place in the annual report the charge appears at all — the consolidated RPT note carries no intra-group flows `[AR FY26, Consolidated Note 36, PDF p223/pr212]`), Management commentary (the MD&A's stated cause), Industry (interoperability is a SEBI construct and W2_industry established that BSE's FY2026 gains came out of NSE rather than out of market growth), Governance (ICCL was served a SEBI show cause notice for non-compliance with the interoperability circular, disposed without penalty on 15-May-2025 `[AR FY26, ICCL Secretarial Audit, PDF p48/pr37]`). Balance sheet: the payable to ICCL rose DERIVED 1,166 / 417 − 1 = 179.6%, faster than the charge.
DOMAINS: IncomeStatement, Notes, MgmtCommentary, Industry, Governance, BalanceSheet
DISCONFIRM: Sought and found, and it cuts substantially against the harshest reading. The intra-group rate per rupee of transaction charge fell (3.65% to 3.44%), so there is no evidence of over-charging; the group's total external clearing cost genuinely fell 19.4%; the Reg 23 materiality test is passed with room (§3.3); and management pay rose 20.5% against PAT up 109.8%, so there is no self-dealing signature anywhere in the ledger. The economics are benign. What fails is the description.
S5 MEANS: The number in the MD&A is true and its stated cause is false, which is a different and more durable defect than a wrong number — the reader is told the group got structurally cheaper at clearing when what actually happened is that it moved clearing from a competitor onto its own balance sheet, and one third of a line the MD&A discusses is a related-party charge the MD&A does not mention. BASE-RATE: The pattern has cost money at this company before: SEBI's Order of 29-Jul-2022 penalised BSE ₹3 lakh under Reg 38(2) SECC for "unrelated / non-incidental activities by BSE or its subsidiaries", set aside by SAT on 2-May-2025 `[AR FY26, CG Report §8B, PDF p94/pr83]`, and ICCL paid ₹5.05 cr on 9-Apr-2025 for control failures inside the same subsidiary `[AR FY25, ICCL Secretarial Audit, PDF p45/pr34]`. Realised loss on the group-structure and clearing-subsidiary axis over the decade is ₹5.05 cr, 0.20% of FY2026 consolidated PAT — demonstrated but immaterial in rupees.
S6 IMPLICATION: The durability of the FY2026 cost improvement rests on clearing-venue market share, which is contestable by the interoperating clearing corporation on the same technology axis BSE used to win it, so a cost line described as structural should be modelled as a share variable with a reversal case rather than as a permanent step down. The monitorable is the FY2027 standalone RPT note's clearing house expense line read against the standalone clearing and settlement expense: if the related-party share rises above one third again while the external portion stops falling, the saving has stopped. Next check: the FY2027 annual report, standalone RPT note. The thesis-breaker is a reversal in members' clearing-venue choice, which would restore the external cost without restoring the intra-group charge to the parent's profit.
SEVERITY: High

S1 WHAT: Form AOC-1 shows ICCL's reserves and surplus falling from 90,781 at 31-Mar-2025 to 47,463 at 31-Mar-2026 — net worth 1,26,181 to 82,863 — in a year the same table reports PAT of 16,979 and a dividend of only 3,540 `[AR FY25, Form AOC-1, PDF p278/pr267; AR FY26, Form AOC-1, PDF p312/pr301]`.

DISCONFIRM: Sought. What has cost money in the same subsidiary is control failure: ₹5.05 cr paid on 9-Apr-2025 for IT asset inventory, audit report submission and primary-to-disaster-recovery correspondence failures `[AR FY25, ICCL Secretarial Audit, PDF p45/pr34]`, which is the same class of defect — something inside ICCL changed and the record did not follow.
S6 IMPLICATION: Any measure of the group's return on shareholders' capital that uses subsidiary net worth from Form AOC-1 must be recomputed on one basis before FY2026 is compared with anything earlier, and the restricted portion — 60,896 of ICCL Core SGF plus 17,952 contributed by BSE itself — should be excluded from the equity base in every year rather than in one. The monitorable is whether the FY2027 Form AOC-1 restates its FY2026 basis again or holds it; next check, the FY2027 annual report. The thesis-breaker for the benign reading would be evidence that the movement is something other than the Core SGF, which ICCL's own s.136 financial statements would settle.

The Schedule III table foots exactly to 2,49,698, which is a genuine internal control passing. The auditor issued an unmodified opinion on both statements and the group's aggregate numbers tie. Nothing here suggests a wrong total; the defect is that entity-level profit cannot be read out of the report.
S5 MEANS: The group publishes two entity-level profit tables that cannot both be used and does not say which is which, so any sum-of-parts, any subsidiary return metric and any judgement about which parts of the tree earn their keep rests on an undisclosed choice — and for the single subsidiary that matters most, the two answers differ by a factor of 2.5. BASE-RATE: The pattern has already produced a documented error in this project rather than a market loss: RF-029 recorded nine ledger cells that silently mixed as-first-reported and as-restated bases, and in every one of the nine the untagged number was the wrong number. No realised financial loss is attributable to entity-table basis confusion at this company or in the peer set.
S6 IMPLICATION: Entity-level profitability in this group is not a fact that can be read; it is a construction, and the earnings-quality gate should treat any per-subsidiary metric as ESTIMATE rather than TRACED until the eliminations are obtained. The margin of safety on any sum-of-parts framing must widen by at least the 11,368 of unexplained parent-level adjustment, which is 4.6% of consolidated profit. The monitorable is whether FY2027 adds an elimination column or a basis note to either table; next check, the FY2027 annual report. The thesis-breaker is a reconciliation from the company that shows the residual is something other than intra-group elimination.
SEVERITY: Medium

### FINDING: CDSL supplies more than all of the group's associate profit and 45.9% of its book equity in unrecognised value, and its dividend vanishes on consolidation
S1 WHAT: The 15% CDSL stake contributed 6,842 of the group's 6,542 total associate profit in FY2026, paid a 3,919 dividend, is carried at 44,940 against a market value of 3,50,932, and appears in the same annual report at three different carrying amounts — 3,541 standalone cost, 23,356 in the consolidated RPT note, 44,940 under the equity method `[AR FY26, Consolidated Note 8 PDF p188–189/pr177–178; Standalone Note 35 PDF p301/pr290; Consolidated Note 36 PDF p226/pr215]`.
S2 WHY: BSE has been a structurally forced seller since the FY2018 deconsolidation — 50.05% at 31-Mar-2017, 20% at 31-Mar-2022, 15% at 31-Mar-2024 `[AR FY17 Form AOC-1 PDF p268/pr266; AR FY22 Form AOC-1 Part B PDF p298/pr289; AR FY24 Form AOC-1 Part B PDF p313/pr307]` — and the residual 15% is now a passive holding accounted for by the equity method, so the market's view of it never touches BSE's reported equity. The 23,356 is most likely a deemed cost set at the FY2018 loss of control, but the report does not say so.
S3 EVIDENCE: DERIVED 6,842 / 6,542 = 104.59% — every other associate together is a net drag of 300. DERIVED 3,50,932 − 44,940 = 3,05,992 of unrecognised value, which is DERIVED 3,05,992 / 6,67,301 = 45.86% of consolidated net worth. The counter-proportion that makes the year's movement legible: CDSL's contribution fell DERIVED 6,842 / 7,900 − 1 = −13.4% and its market value fell DERIVED 3,50,932 / 3,82,486 − 1 = −8.2%, while the cash BSE took out rose DERIVED 3,919 / 3,449 − 1 = +13.6%, taking the received payout ratio from DERIVED 43.7% to 57.3%. On the cash flow, the standalone ties exactly — DERIVED 3,540 + 78 + 1,000 + 1,500 + 3,919 + 1 = 10,038 — while consolidated investing carries "Dividend received 1" and the 3,919 appears nowhere `[AR FY26, Standalone Cash Flow PDF p251/pr240; Consolidated Cash Flow PDF p165/pr154]`, in FY2026 and again in FY2025, DERIVED 3,919 + 3,449 = 7,368 over two years.
S4 CONNECTS: Balance sheet (carrying versus market), Cash flow (the missing associate dividend), Notes (three carrying values and no Ind AS 112 summarised information for the group's most material associate, though it is given for the smaller India ICC at PDF p187/pr176), Governance (BSE is CDSL's promoter, nominates to its board, shares the S.R. Batliboi audit relationship established by W2_peers, and buys its own AGM e-voting service from it `[AR FY26, Notice of the 21st AGM, PDF p12]`), Business (a depository is the natural counterparty of an exchange, so the absence of any described commercial flow beyond 285 of "administrative and other expenses" is itself information).
DOMAINS: BalanceSheet, CashFlow, Notes, Governance, Business
DISCONFIRM: Sought. The equity method is the correct treatment for a 15% holding with significant influence and there is no discretion to mark it to market, so the unrecognised value is a feature of the standard rather than a choice by management. The dividend classification error is small — 1.26% of consolidated CFO — and does not affect closing cash or profit. The stake reduction to 15% was regulatory, not opportunistic, and delivered two of the largest realised gains in the company's history, ₹9,158 lakh in FY2020 and ₹50,417 lakh standalone in FY2024.
S5 MEANS: Nearly half of BSE's consolidated book equity is a number that has already been earned and is not on the balance sheet, and the single most material associate in the group is the one for which the group provides the least analysable disclosure — no summarised financials, no described commercial relationship, and a carrying value that appears three ways in one document. BASE-RATE: The stake has produced realised cash rather than loss — ₹9,158 lakh and ₹50,417 lakh of standalone exceptional gains on the two sell-downs, and ₹3,919 lakh of dividend in FY2026 alone. The demonstrated risk on this axis is not credit or valuation; it is that a regulator can compel a further reduction, which it has twice.
S6 IMPLICATION: Any equity-value framing of this group must treat the CDSL stake separately from the operating business, because 45.9% of reported book equity is a passive quoted holding whose market value moved 8.2% the other way from its carrying value in FY2026, and the earnings contribution and the cash contribution moved in opposite directions in the same year. The monitorable is the received payout ratio: 57.3% in FY2026 against 43.7% in FY2025, with the next check at the FY2027 associate roll-forward. The thesis-breaker is a further regulator-driven reduction below 15%, which would convert a recurring equity-accounted contribution into a one-off gain and remove 2.7% of consolidated profit permanently.
SEVERITY: Medium

### FINDING: India INX is ten years of money going in and nothing coming back, carried at 2.6x its underlying net assets behind a KAM that cites the wrong note
S1 WHAT: BSE has put 20,919 of equity into India International Exchange (IFSC) Limited across FY2017–FY2026, including 4,128 in FY2026, has never received a dividend, and carries the stake at cost less a 1,511 provision `[AR FY26, Standalone Note 7, PDF p267/pr256]` against an entity with an accumulated deficit of 20,588 and ten consecutive loss years `[AR FY26, Form AOC-1, PDF p312/pr301]`.
S2 WHY: The entity is the group's IFSC exchange at GIFT City. Its FY2026 turnover is 233 against a loss before tax of 2,580, so the losses are not a scaling problem but an absence of revenue — the business has not found a fee base in ten years. BSE keeps funding it because an IFSC exchange licence requires maintained net worth, which is why each injection coincides with a rise in BSE's holding percentage rather than with a change in the business.
S3 EVIDENCE: DERIVED 5,500 + 2,000 + 5,000 + 2,055 + 2,236 + 4,128 = 20,919 injected; DERIVED 942 + 1,968 + 3,163 + 3,136 + 3,168 + 3,022 + 2,985 + 1,571 + 1,453 + 2,615 = 24,023 of cumulative loss on a 100% basis; dividends across the decade, 0. The counter-proportion: the provision is DERIVED 1,511 / 20,919 = 7.22% of cost, while the entity's net assets attributable to BSE are DERIVED (32,050 − 20,588) × 65.27% = 7,481, so the net carrying value of 19,408 is DERIVED 19,408 / 7,481 = 2.59x the underlying net-asset share. Against this, the standalone audit's one substantive key audit matter is "Valuation of investments in subsidiary and associate and its impairment (as described in Note 32 of the standalone financial statements)" `[AR FY26, Standalone Auditor's Report, PDF p241/pr230]` — and standalone Note 32 is "Financial Instruments" `[AR FY26, Standalone Note 32, PDF p290/pr279]`, which contains no impairment disclosure. No note in the standalone financial statements gives an impairment assumption, discount rate, forecast horizon or recoverable amount.
S4 CONNECTS: Balance sheet (a carrying value 2.6x underlying net assets), Notes (the provision note and the absent impairment note), Cash flow (4,128 of cash out in FY2026 against nil in), Governance (the auditor raised this as its only substantive KAM and cross-referenced a note that does not describe it; the consolidated audit raises no KAM on it at all `[AR FY26, Consolidated Auditor's Report, PDF p157/pr146]`), Business (an exchange with turnover of 233 against BSE's 4,46,951 is not a business in any operating sense).
DOMAINS: BalanceSheet, Notes, CashFlow, Governance, Business
DISCONFIRM: Sought and partly found. Ind AS 36 tests recoverable amount as the higher of value in use and fair value less costs of disposal, not against net assets, so a carrying value above net assets is not itself an impairment indicator — a licensed IFSC exchange with regulatory scarcity value can be worth more than its book. The auditor did examine the matter, listed specific procedures including assessing management's evaluation of impairment indicators "by considering the financial performance and net worth position of the subsidiaries", and issued an unmodified opinion. India INX also generated other comprehensive income of 859 in FY2026 on translation. The amount at risk, 19,408, is 7.8% of consolidated PAT and 2.9% of consolidated net worth.
S5 MEANS: The group has funded a loss-making licence for ten years without ever stating what it is worth or why, and the one control that was supposed to answer that question — a key audit matter — points the reader at a note that does not exist for this purpose, so the single largest judgemental carrying value in the standalone balance sheet is supported by no disclosure at all. BASE-RATE: The loss has already been realised at the entity level — 24,023 of cumulative losses is real money spent — and the parent has recognised only 1,511 of it. Within this group the precedent for eventual write-down exists: investments in BSE CSR Integrated Foundation and BFSI Sector Skill Council were "fully provided for" `[AR FY26, Standalone Note 7, PDF p268/pr257]`, and a 1,511 provision was taken against this same entity in FY2025. The pattern of funding a subsidiary to a full write-off has occurred at this company, at small scale.
S6 IMPLICATION: The margin of safety on the standalone balance sheet should absorb the gap between the 19,408 carried and the 7,481 of underlying net assets, i.e. up to 11,927 or 4.8% of FY2026 consolidated PAT, because nothing in the report demonstrates that the difference is recoverable. The monitorable is the provision for diminution line in the standalone investment note read against India INX's turnover: a further injection without a turnover inflection is the signal. Next check, the FY2027 standalone Note 7. The thesis-breaker is a disclosed value-in-use calculation showing a recoverable amount above cost, or an IFSC revenue line that finally scales.
SEVERITY: Medium

### FINDING: The supervision of investment advisers moved out of a standalone subsidiary and into the fee-earning parent, and its fee stream is not disclosed
S1 WHAT: SEBI recognised BSE Limited itself as Research Analyst Administration and Supervisory Body and Investment Adviser Administration and Supervisory Body for five years from 25-Jul-2024 `[AR FY26, MD&A, PDF p65/pr54]`; BSE inspected 84 Investment Advisers and 71 Research Analysts in FY2026 `[AR FY26, MD&A, PDF p64/pr53]`; BSE Administration & Supervision Limited, whose stated principal activity was "Administration and supervision of SEBI Registered Advisers" `[AR FY26, Consolidated Note 40.1, PDF p171/pr160]`, transferred 529 of annual membership and subscription fees to BSE in FY2025 `[AR FY26, Standalone Note 35, PDF p297/pr286]` and was merged into BSE Technologies Private Limited with appointed date 1-Apr-2025 `[AR FY26, Board's Report, PDF p28/pr17]`.
S2 WHY: The reorganisation follows SEBI's own 12-Jul-2024 circular, which granted the recognition to BSE Limited rather than to a subsidiary, so the parent had to house the function. The residual shell was then folded into the group's IT-services company rather than wound up. The structural consequence is that the supervisor and the fee recipient are now the same legal entity, and the entity's other business is selling services to the population it supervises.
S3 EVIDENCE: The fee stream is not separately disclosed. Standalone revenue from operations breaks into transaction charges 3,66,948, listing services 51,893 and other operating income 28,110, within which the only candidate lines are "Annual subscription and processing fees 4,448" and "Other fees 2,620" `[AR FY26, Standalone Note 22, PDF p285/pr274]`. The counter-proportion that sizes the conflict at its widest: listing services, paid by the issuers BSE supervises as an exchange, are DERIVED 51,893 / 4,46,951 = 11.61% of standalone operating revenue, while the adviser-supervision fees are not separable at all — 0% of the revenue note is attributable to a function that produced 155 inspections. The segment note reports one segment `[AR FY26, Standalone Note 38, PDF p303/pr292]`. BASL's own last full year shows the scale that has been absorbed: turnover 248, PAT 139, net assets 828 `[AR FY25, Form AOC-1, PDF p278/pr267]`.
S4 CONNECTS: Business (the exchange now regulates and bills two populations — issuers and advisers), Governance (SEBI penalised BSE ₹3 lakh on 29-Jul-2022 under Reg 38(2) SECC for "unrelated / non-incidental activities by BSE or its subsidiaries", set aside by SAT on 2-May-2025 — the same day the training subsidiaries were disposed of `[AR FY26, CG Report §8B, PDF p94/pr83]`), Notes (the RPT note is the only record of the fee transfer from BASL), Industry (W1.7 §4.3 identifies "the company regulates its own customers" as an MII-specific governance condition, and this is that condition widening rather than narrowing).
DOMAINS: Business, Governance, Notes, Industry
DISCONFIRM: Sought and substantial. SEBI chose the structure — the recognition was granted to BSE Limited, not sought by it in preference to a subsidiary — and SEBI supervises the supervisor, with W2_gov_early establishing that public interest directors are SEBI-approved and six of eight board seats are PIDs. The amounts are trivial: BASL's entire turnover was 248 in its last full year, 0.06% of standalone operating revenue. The group also removed a layer of complexity that SEBI had itself objected to in 2022. Nothing here shows a supervisory failure; the finding is about disclosure and structure, not conduct.
S5 MEANS: The conflict W1.7 identifies as structural to an MII widened in FY2026 — from issuers only to issuers plus investment advisers and research analysts — at the same moment the arm's-length subsidiary that had held the adviser function was dissolved into a commercial IT company, and the revenue from the newly absorbed supervisory role cannot be located in the financial statements at all. BASE-RATE: The regulatory function at this company has produced every penalty in the period: ₹3 cr on Karvy supervision (stayed), ₹25 lakh on dissemination and error-account monitoring (paid), ₹5.05 cr at ICCL on IT controls (paid). Realised loss ₹5.30 cr across the decade, 0.21% of FY2026 consolidated PAT — small, but it is the only function at BSE with a loss history at all, and RF-001 records that it still has no board-level owner.
S6 IMPLICATION: The regulatory-cost line — W1.7 §3's "cost of the regulatory function itself", to be tracked as a percentage of revenue rather than in rupees — is now less measurable than it was a year ago, because the function's revenue and its cost are both inside an entity that reports one segment, and an exchange that under-funds supervision is exchanging near-term margin for regulatory risk that has already crystallised three times. The monitorable is whether the FY2027 revenue note breaks out adviser-supervision fees or whether the SEBI-mandated Executive Directors for the regulatory verticals are appointed, either of which would restore observability. Next check, the FY2027 annual report and corporate governance report. The thesis-breaker is a SEBI action against BSE in its RAASB or IAASB capacity, which would convert a structural observation into a demonstrated failure.
SEVERITY: Medium
