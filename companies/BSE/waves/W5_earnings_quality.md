## 1. THE CLASSIFICATION FRAME

The orchestrator's three categories are used throughout, because they carry different weight and the
register conflates them in places:

| | Category | What it means | What it does to valuation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | **The numbers are unreliable** | The reported amount would change if the item were restated | Blocks valuation. This is what the gate exists to detect |
| **(b)** | **Reliable but incompletely disclosed** | The amount is right; you cannot see inside it | Widens the margin of safety; does not block |
| **(c)** | **Reliable and disclosed, but the economics are not what the caption implies** | Every input is published; the label is doing work the substance does not support | Changes *which number* the model uses; does not block |

**The reading tested.** The orchestrator's prior is that most of this register is (b) and (c), not (a).
**That reading survives the test, and the test was run to break it.** §5.2 sizes every candidate for
(a) in rupees and against PAT; the largest defensible aggregate is **5.15% of PAT attributable**, and
more than half of that is a timing item with explicit disconfirming evidence. The three places where
the register comes closest to (a) — the FY2025 receivable comparative, the standalone Note 30 EPS
row, and the ₹5,250 lakh bridge plug — are recorded as (a) at their sizes and none of them touches
FY2026 profit.

## 2. THE TEN TESTS

### Test 1 — Accrual versus cash profit

**Result: (c), decisively. Not (a).**

`DERIVED` from `W4_cash.md` §3.3, each year read from its own annual report:
`cumulative reported CFO 8,24,646 − Core SGF add-backs 70,175 − cumulative float movement 2,92,958 =
own-account CFO 4,61,513`; against `cumulative PAT total operations 6,48,302`, a conversion of
`4,61,513 / 6,48,302 = 0.712`. Headline `8,24,646 / 6,48,302 = 1.272`
`[AR FY2017, Note 18, PDF p170/pr168]` through `[AR FY26, Note 16, PDF p206/pr195]`.
Cumulative float telescopes and is checkable in one line: `DERIVED: 3,55,423 − 62,465 = 2,92,958`.

**What it means for the reliability of reported profit: nothing.** The 0.712 is not a statement about
PAT. It is a statement about the *numerator*. `DERIVED: 2,92,958 / 8,24,646 = 35.53%` of a decade of
headline operating cash is clearing-member float and `70,175 / 8,24,646 = 8.51%` is Core SGF flows on
a basis the company itself abandoned in FY2025 — together **44.0%**. Reported PAT is unaffected by
either. The two maps in `W4_cash.md` §2.4 make the point arithmetically: **profit differs by ₹2.66 per
₹100 of revenue and cash differs by ₹20.20**, and `DERIVED: 17.54 / 20.20 = 86.8%` of the cash gap is
one working-capital line whose *sign flips* between the two presentations.

**What it does NOT mean, stated precisely because the register invites the wrong reading.**

- **Float is not fraud.** Note 16 splits owned / earmarked / clearing-and-settlement by name in **all
  ten** annual reports, and every year's (A)+(B)+(C) ties to that year's balance-sheet caption exactly
  `[AR FY26, Note 16, PDF p206/pr195]`. Note 12.1 states in terms which balances are restricted
  `[AR FY26, Note 12.1, PDF p202/pr191]`. Nothing is concealed; the separation SEBI requires on the
  balance sheet is made in full, and there is no equivalent cash-flow requirement.
- **A clearing house holding member money is the business model working as designed.** BSE
  consolidates ICCL because SEBI requires it to own the clearing node; member margin and settlement
  obligations are the mechanism by which clearing works, not an anomaly in it. No peer publishes an
  own-account CFO — the correction is an analyst construction, not a compliance finding
  (`W4_cash.md` F4 DISCONFIRM iii).
- **Presenting collected tax inside operating cash flow is standard.** Ind AS 7 does not require
  statutory remittances to be excluded, and most Indian filers present them exactly as BSE does.

**What survives.** Reported CFO is a measure of how much of other people's money was in the group's
bank accounts on the reporting date, and in FY2026 `DERIVED: 24,251 + 44,937 = 69,188` of the
₹72,767 lakh working-capital line — **95.1%** — was members' float and government tax, against
₹3,579 lakh (4.9%) of the company's own working capital. That is a caption problem of the first
order, and it is (c).

**Base rate — has this pattern cost money?** Not in cash: the float has never defaulted and no clearing
member default has been charged against ICCL's Core SGF in ten years. It has cost *measurement*, five
times, on the record: bucket (C) fell in FY2018, FY2019, FY2021, FY2023 and FY2025 by 26,468, 34,863,
19,322, 31,928 and 26,993, and in each of those years reported CFO went negative or near-zero while
profit stayed positive. And it cost this run: three internal artifacts reached three different
conversions from the same ten filings (1.264 → 0.942 → 0.712), and **each error ran in the company's
favour.**

### Test 2 — Revenue recognition

**These are two findings and they must not be conflated. Recognition: clean. Disclosure: materially
deficient. (a) on neither; (b) on the second.**

**(i) Is recognition itself aggressive? No — and the policy is the least discretionary model
available.** Transaction charges are recognised *"at a point in time as an when the transaction
occurs… measured as per the specified rate"* `[AR FY26, Note 2.3.10, PDF p176-177/pr165-166]`. There
is **no variable consideration, no over-time measurement and no estimate in the trigger** — the trade
either occurred on the platform or it did not, and the rate is filed with SEBI. The policy is
**unchanged** between FY2025 and FY2026, and no accounting-policy change could account for any
FY2026 movement (`W3_revenue.md` §5.3, Test 3). This is the single strongest fact in favour of the
company anywhere in the register, and it applies to 78.51% of revenue.

The receivable question that would have contradicted it resolves the same way. On Q4-annualised
revenue gross receivable days are **38.0 (FY2026) against 37.5 (FY2025) — flat**
`DERIVED: Q4FY26 revenue 1,56,351 against Q4FY25 84,664 = +84.7%`
`[Q4FY26 Results 07May2026, consolidated quarterly filing]`. The live receivable cycle has
*shortened* over two years, 32.0 days against 38.9. Invoiced-and-current days fell `17.4 → 3.2 → 2.8`
and write-offs fell `827 → 452 → 256` `[AR FY26, Note 10.4, PDF p199/pr188]`. The verdict of three
independent agents (W3_notes, W3_revenue, W3_balancesheet) is **timing, not recognition**, and the
adjudicated contradiction between them closed in W3_notes' favour on the fact and in W3_revenue's on
the framing — both populations are true and arithmetically compatible.

What survives on the recognition side is narrow and is carried at its size: a genuine lengthening of
**₹9,669 lakh — 2.00% of revenue** (`W3_revenue.md` §5.3, Test 2), or ₹14,300 lakh (5.7% of PAT) on
W3_notes' full-extension measure. Both positions stand; the difference is which base-year growth
assumption is used. That is **(c) at worst**, and it is inside the stated 0-to-60-day credit period on
a book where the counterparty has already posted collateral `[AR FY26, Note 34, PDF p219/pr208]`.

**(ii) Is the *disclosure* deficient? Yes, and it is graded against a peer set that is unanimous.**
The audited Note 22 stops immediately above the only line that matters:

| Note 22 component | ₹ lakh | % of revenue | Audited sub-split |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Transaction charges — A | 3,79,499 | **78.51%** | **NONE — one line** |
| Listing services — B | 51,903 | 10.74% | 3 ways |
| Treasury income on clearing and settlement funds — C | 17,142 | 3.55% | named, no split |
| Other operating income — D | 34,851 | 7.21% | **6 ways** |

`DERIVED: 3,79,499 + 51,903 + 17,142 + 34,851 = 4,83,395`, zero residual
`[AR FY26, Note 22, PDF p211/pr200]`.

**The asymmetry inside one note is the finding (§1a-i).** *Auction charges* of ₹60 lakh —
`DERIVED: 60 / 4,83,395 = 0.012%` of revenue — carry their own audited caption while 78.51% of revenue
does not. **The granularity is not absent; it is allocated away from the only line that decides the
case.** The counter-proportion: the six sub-lines of Other operating income average
`DERIVED: 34,851 / 6 = 5,809` lakh apiece, and the unsplit transaction-charge line is
`DERIVED: 3,79,499 / 5,809 = 65×` that average.

**Every explanation is eliminated by one control.** NSE's transaction charges are **78.65%** of its
revenue against BSE's 78.51% — a 0.14pp gap — and NSE splits that identical line five ways *inside the
audited note*, Options alone ₹9,997.57 cr `[NSE AR FY26, Note 27, PDF p159-160/pr314-317]`.
Concentration, business model, regulator, statute **and auditor** are therefore all eliminated. On the
Ind AS 115 timing disaggregation the peer set is **4 of 4 against BSE** — NSE 13,456.94/3,144.37, MCX
2,103.70/198.30, CDSL 67,317.01/47,174.86, IEX 58,771.57/2,793.13 (`W2_peers.md` §5).

And the de facto contract balances exist and are unlabelled: unbilled ₹50,637 lakh + income received in
advance `2,901 + 1,753` + advance from customers ₹4,475 lakh
`DERIVED: 50,637 + 2,901 + 1,753 + 4,475 = 59,766`, i.e. `59,766 / 4,83,395 = 12.36%` of revenue
`[AR FY26, Note 19, PDF p208/pr197]` and `[AR FY26, Note 10.5, PDF p199/pr188]`.

> **The consequence for the gate.** The ~70% equity-derivatives concentration that carries the entire
> investment case is **attested at the competitor and MD&A-only at BSE** — an unaudited numerator over
> an unaudited standalone denominator of 4,46,951 against the audited consolidated 4,83,395. That is
> **(b)**, it is the largest single (b) in the file, and it is a constraint on W8 rather than a bar to it.

**Base rate.** No revenue restatement has ever occurred at this company. R-09 (FY2018) moved ₹3,022
lakh between revenue and other income with total income unchanged; R-01/R-14 are Ind AS 105
discontinued-operations reclassifications with PAT attributable unchanged. **Zero realised loss and
zero restatement through the recognition channel in ten years.**

### Test 3 — One-off and exceptional frequency

**Result: (c). The numbers are right; the caption "exceptional" is not.**

A below-the-line non-operating item appears in **7 of 10 years** — FY2017, FY2018, FY2019, FY2020,
FY2021, FY2024, FY2026. Only FY2022, FY2023 and FY2025 are clean, on both bases
(`W4_belowline.md` §6.2). And the composition is one asset:
`DERIVED: gross positive standalone exceptional income 31,556 + 457 + 9,158 + 50,417 + 1,590 = 93,178`;
`DERIVED: CDSL tranches 31,556 + 9,158 + 50,417 = 91,131`; `91,131 / 93,178 = 97.80%`
`[AR FY18, MD&A, PDF p17/pr10]` · `[AR FY20, MD&A, PDF p118/pr111]` ·
`[AR FY24, Consolidated P&L, PDF p157/pr151]` · `[AR FY26, Standalone P&L, PDF p250/pr239]`.

**The counter-proportion that makes it a finding rather than a note.** The stake was sold
50.05% → 24% → 20% → 15%, and **only the first tranche was compelled** (SEBI letter
MRD/DSA/OW/9183/1/2017). Two of the three were management decisions. So the category is better
modelled as a **divestment programme** than as chance — and what remains of it, the residual 15%, is
carried at `DERIVED: 44,940 / 3,50,932 = 12.81%` of its own disclosed market value
`[AR FY26, Note 8, PDF p188-189/pr177-178]`.

**Disconfirming, and it is substantial.** Each disposal is separately captioned, quantified and
explained in the year it occurs; nothing was disguised as operating income; the FY2024 consolidated
P&L segregates ₹40,662 lakh on its own face line; and BSE consistently presents EPS both before and
after exceptional items, so the underlying number is always available. The FY2021 item ran the *other*
way — a ₹1,453 lakh S&P Dow Jones arbitration loss `[AR FY21, MD&A, PDF p102/pr97]`.

**Base rate.** Realised repeatedly and always as a gain on this asset: FY2018 31,556, FY2020 9,158,
FY2024 50,417 standalone. No loss and no impairment has ever been taken against the CDSL holding. The
one realised loss in the category is the ₹1,453 lakh arbitration award, and it arose from a contract
dispute rather than from a disposal.

### Test 4 — Provisioning adequacy and reversals

**Result: NOT a finding against the company on adequacy. (b) on the disclosure of the assumption
change. This is where the register most needs the disconfirming evidence applied.**

The two movements the register flags are real:
- the under-6-month expected credit loss rate cut **39% → 13% consolidated** and **47% → 13%
  standalone**, with **no reason given in either block** `[AR FY26, Note 10.5, PDF p199-200/pr188-189]`
  and `[AR FY26, Note 10.1, PDF p276-277/pr265-266]`;
- the parent releasing **₹284 lakh** while the group's allowance rose **62.6%**
  `DERIVED: 6,024 − 256 + 4,025 = 9,793` ✓.

**Both are explained, and the explanation is on the record.** Management stated it directly: *"there
was a provision of about Rs. 40 crores made in our subsidiary clearing company from one of the
debtors"* `[Q1FY27 Concall 10Aug2026, CFO]`. That matches the consolidated impairment charge of 4,025
against a standalone **release** of (284) to the rupee-order. `W3_balancesheet.md` §4 establishes it is
**one identified counterparty at the clearing subsidiary — not a pattern**, and the allowance
reconciles exactly and ties to the cash flow within ₹32 lakh on a ₹30,595 lakh movement (0.1%).

**And the re-estimate is net conservative.** Applying FY2025's full loss matrix to FY2026's buckets
gives **8,914 against an actual 9,793** — the group provided **₹879 lakh more** than the old matrix
required (`W3_balancesheet.md` F3 DISCONFIRM iii). The 39%→13% cut in one bucket is more than offset by
taking the 1–2 year bucket from 46% to **100%**, and every bucket beyond one year is 100% provided in
FY2026 against 46% and 99% in FY2025. **The older book got more conservative, not less.** Isolated, the
under-6-month cut is worth `DERIVED: 809` lakh of charge foregone — `809 / 2,49,698 = 0.32%` of PAT
attributable.

**What survives, and it is the whole of it:** a material ECL assumption changed and no reason was
given. The only method disclosure is *"a practical expedient by computing the expected credit loss
allowance for trade receivables based on a detailed analysis of trade receivable by individual
departments"*, which describes a method and not a change of assumption, and which is not the
Ind AS 109.B5.5.35 provision matrix. **That is (b), at 0.32% of PAT.**
`NOT-DISCLOSED -> the basis for the FY2026 under-6-month ECL rate revision; only BSE's board-approved
ECL model paper, which is unpublished, or an FY2027 note giving the revised matrix by counterparty
class, would answer it.`

**Base rate.** BSE's receivable book *does* produce realised losses, routinely and at scale — listing
fees billed to suspended and non-compliant listed companies are structurally uncollectible, and
₹1,535 lakh was written off across three years `DERIVED: 827 + 452 + 256 = 1,535`, i.e.
`1,535 / 2,49,698 = 0.61%` of one year's PAT, **falling** as a rate (3.46% → 1.22% → 0.39% of gross).
The realised base rate is established, it is high in rate terms, and it is *why* a 67.7% ECL on the
billed book is credible rather than alarming.

### Test 5 — Capitalisation

**Result: (b) with a bounded (c). Not (a).**

Two items:

**(i) The expense-versus-capitalise boundary moved 14.2pp in one year.** Capitalised hardware as a
share of total technology outlay went `DERIVED: 8,279 / 24,708 = 33.51%` to
`18,530 / 38,843 = 47.71%`, **+14.20pp** `[AR FY26, Note 3, PDF p181/pr170]` and
`[AR FY26, Note 6, PDF p185/pr174]`. Reversion to the FY2025 share would move roughly **₹5,518 lakh**
from the balance sheet into the expense line — `DERIVED: 5,518 / 2,49,698 = 2.21%` of PAT attributable.

**Disconfirming, and it is strong.** (a) The shift is in **hardware**, governed by Ind AS 16, not in
capitalised development cost under Ind AS 38 — software intangible additions were only ₹2,470 lakh and
net software carrying value barely moved, 3,893 → 4,122. (b) There is a visible revenue counterpart:
standalone colocation income more than doubled to ₹16,899 lakh from ₹7,262 lakh, **+133%**. (c) The
capex is demonstrably real and capitalised rather than parked — PPE rose 22,217 → 56,259 while CWIP
**fell** 4,073 → 3,255. The finding is therefore about **disclosure adequacy and the forward
depreciation tail, not about capitalisation impropriety**, and W3_cost said so first.
`NOT-DISCLOSED -> the capitalisation threshold policy; Ind AS 1.97 would require the break-up, and the
FY2027 Note 26 is the settling document.`

**(ii) RF-046 — the capital advance ran through the wrong section in both directions.** A capital
advance of **nil (FY2024) → 15,584 (FY2025) → nil (FY2026)** — larger than FY2025's entire reported
capex line of 15,323 — was **paid through FY2025 operating** and **reported as FY2026 investing capex
while being released through FY2026 operating** `[AR FY26, Note 13, PDF p202/pr191]` and
`[AR FY25, Note 13, PDF p188/pr177]`.

**It is a classification error, and it self-reverses.** `DERIVED: corrected FY2026 cash capex
51,516 − 15,584 = 35,932; corrected FY2025 15,323 + 15,584 = 30,907; step-up 35,932 / 30,907 = 1.16×,
not 51,516 / 15,323 = 3.36×`. The two-year total is unaffected either way:
`DERIVED: 15,323 + 51,516 = 66,839 = 30,907 + 35,932`. **The three-year cumulative conversion is
unchanged** — the error was self-reversing across the pair, which is exactly why no single-year check
would find it and why the cumulative test the doctrine mandates did not either.

**Base rate.** No realised loss from technology capitalisation at BSE or in the peer set: no impairment
of capitalised technology in any of the ten years, no write-off of CWIP, and FY2026's only goodwill
impairment is **₹4 lakh** `[AR FY26, Note 28, PDF p213/pr202]`. Recorded as **absent**.

### Test 6 — Depreciation

**Result: (b). A checkability defect with a bounded modelling consequence. Not (a), and the direction
of the change is conservative.**

Note 2.3.5(b) prints **"Buildings* 60 years"** in the table of estimated useful lives; Note 3 footnote
3, two pages later, states the Holding Company *"has revised the estimated useful life of its building
to 25 years, effective April 01, 2024 impact of same was Rs. 13"*
`[AR FY26, Note 2.3.5(b), PDF p174/pr163]` and `[AR FY26, Note 3 fn 3, PDF p181/pr170]`. **The FY2025
report carries the identical pair** `[AR FY25, Note 2.2.5, PDF p166/pr155]` and
`[AR FY25, Note 3 fn 3, PDF p172/pr161]` — two audited reports, two years running, two answers.

**Sized.** The difference in rate is `DERIVED: 25-year WDV rate 1 − 0.05^(1/25) = 11.29%` against
`60-year 1 − 0.05^(1/60) = 4.87%` — a factor of 2.32. On the ₹18,022 lakh of FY2026 building additions
that is a first-full-year difference of `DERIVED: 18,022 × (11.29% − 4.87%) = 1,157` lakh, i.e.
`1,157 / 2,49,698 = 0.46%` of PAT attributable. The counter-proportion that matters: the disclosed
impact of ₹13 lakh is `DERIVED: 13 / 15,896 = 0.08%` of the total charge, while the class it applies to
now carries `21,970 / 1,05,539 = 20.82%` of the group's gross block against `3,849 / 63,738 = 6.04%` a
year earlier — **a policy footnote sized against last year's asset base and left standing against this
year's.**

**Disconfirming, and it is decisive on intent.** (i) The asterisk explicitly directs the reader to Note
3, so the conflict is signposted rather than concealed. (ii) A shorter life is the **conservative**
direction — it accelerates the charge and reduces reported profit, the opposite of the incentive that
would make this worth suspecting. (iii) The implied FY2026 rate **is** consistent with 25 years:
`DERIVED: 1,269 / (1,778 + 18,022 / 2) = 11.76%` against the 25-year rate of 11.29%. **The charge
actually taken looks right.** What survives is that a reader cannot tell which buildings run on which
life and cannot reproduce the stated ₹13 lakh, because the note never states the life revised *from*.
`NOT-DISCLOSED -> the useful life buildings were revised from, and which properties the external
valuation covered; the FY2027 Note 3 footnote is the settling document.`

**Base rate.** No realised loss from a useful-life estimate at BSE in the ten-year corpus — no
impairment of property has ever been taken. The base rate for this specific failure mode at this
company is **zero occurrences**, and the finding is about checkability rather than demonstrated loss.

### Test 7 — Other-income dependence

**Result: (c), and it is the sharpest (c) in the file. Not (a) — every number is right.**

**The level.** Investment income is ₹29,030 lakh, ₹6.01 of the ₹100 map. Treasury income on the
clearing float is ₹17,142 lakh, ₹3.55 more, booked inside *revenue from operations*. Together
`DERIVED: 29,030 + 17,142 = 46,172` — **and it fell**, against `25,470 + 21,778 = 47,248`,
`DERIVED: 46,172 − 47,248 = −1,076`, `−1,076 / 47,248 = −2.28%`, on a combined earning base that grew
`DERIVED: 8,31,882 / 6,84,266 = 1.216`, **+21.6%**. Blended yield `46,172 / 8,31,882 = 5.551%` against
`47,248 / 6,84,266 = 6.905%`, **−1.354pp**. **No page of the annual report shows this**, because the
two halves sit in different places in the P&L.

**Whose money.** `DERIVED: 17,142 / 46,172 = 37.13%` of all investment-type income reaching the P&L is
earned on Note 16 bucket (C), members' money, and `17,142 / 2,48,725 = 6.89%` of group PAT. The
counter-proportion: the float is `3,43,298 / 8,31,882 = 41.27%` of the earning base but supplies only
37.13% of the income, because it yields `17,142 / 3,43,298 = 4.99%` against the own corpus's 5.942%.
**No policy paragraph covers it in either direction** — policy 2.3.2 credits earmarked income to the
liability, policy 2.3.12 credits Core SGF income to the fund, and the Ind AS 115 revenue policy 2.3.10
names eleven streams and omits this one `[AR FY26, Note 2.3.10, PDF p176-177/pr165-166]`.
`NOT-DISCLOSED -> the recognition policy for treasury income on clearing and settlement funds; only an
FY2027 addition to Note 2.3.10, or ICCL's own collateral-investment mandate filed with SEBI, would
answer it.`

**The increment — RF-053.** In the first quarter after the audited accounts, consolidated revenue from
operations grew `DERIVED: 1,56,602 − 1,56,351 = 251`, **+0.16% sequentially**, while PBT rose 10,021.
The bridge ties to the rupee with zero residual: investment income **+7,349 (73.3%)**, non-repeat of the
₹40 crore clearing-subsidiary provision **+3,687 (36.8%)**, operating top line **+251 (2.5%)**
`[Q1FY27 Results 04Aug2026, consolidated quarterly filing, PDF p5]`.
`DERIVED: (7,349 + 3,687) / 10,021 = 110.1%` — **the two non-operating items are 110.1% of the profit
increase.**

Management presented it as *"the 14th consecutive quarter of record revenues… Rs. 1,707 crores"*
`[Q1FY27 Concall 10Aug2026, MD & CEO]`. That figure is **total income**, and

## 5. THE VERDICT

### 5.1 Why not "Cannot be relied upon"

The gate fails only on category (a). Five things establish that (a) is not the case here, and the first
four are **affirmative evidence, not the absence of evidence**:

1. **Five independent reconstructions of the FY2026 accounts close.** The own-account cash construction
   against the balance sheet: **₹49 lakh, 0.02%** on ₹2,43,208 lakh. The five operating working-capital
   lines against their notes: **₹398 lakh, 0.13%** of CFO. The consolidated cash flow's own footing:
   exact at every subtotal. Note 22's eleven components against the P&L: **zero residual**. The tax
   charge: `DERIVED: 82,590 + 256 − 462 = 82,384`, zero residual. PPE gross and accumulated
   depreciation, the ECL roll-forward, the Core SGF contributor matrix, the FY2024 restatement's three
   subtotals and the CARO disputed-dues table against the contingent-liability note — **every one ties.**
2. **The restatement base rate is low and the restatements attach to identifiable events, not a habit.**
   Twenty-six movements across the corpus — compiled by `W3_notes.md` §2.1 on the nine annual reports
   readable before GAP-001 closed, and not revisited since — contain exactly **two** earnings
   restatements: R-05
   (FY2018 PAT 71,128 → 68,872, **−2,256, −3.17%**), which is **stated and quantified** in FY2019 Note
   30 fn 2, and R-25 (FY2026 EPS 96.55 → 32.18), which is mechanical and correct under Ind AS 33
   (`DERIVED: 96.55 / 3 = 32.18`). Everything else is reclassification, presentation or
   basis-of-preparation. **FY2020, FY2021, FY2022, FY2023 and FY2025 carry no earnings restatement at
   all.** The two events behind the material movements are the FY2017 CDSL deconsolidation under Ind AS
   105 and the FY2018–FY2019 Core SGF reclassification — both named, both explained.
3. **Four defects run against the company's interest, which is the signature earnings management does
   not leave.** The FY2024 CFO restatement **cut** reported CFO by ₹19,857 lakh. The ECL re-estimate is
   **net conservative by ₹879 lakh**. The buildings useful life moved to the **shorter** figure. The
   headline EPS management leads with is the **lower** number (₹56.27 against an audited ₹60.61). Add
   the voluntary Core SGF policy, which permanently reduces reported profit by ₹11,279 lakh a year at
   policy rate. **A register of five conservative errors is not a register of a company managing
   earnings.**
4. **The recognition model has almost no discretion in it.** Point in time, at a specified rate filed
   with SEBI, no variable consideration, no over-time measurement, policy unchanged — on 78.51% of
   revenue. The single largest source of accrual manipulation in the general case is simply not
   available here.
5. **The clean audit and CARO record.** Nine consecutive unmodified opinions with one emphasis of
   matter (FY2020, quantified at ₹1,264 lakh, resolved). CARO FY2026: **no fraud noticed or reported**
   (xi(a)); **no ADT-4 filed** (xi(b)); **no auditor resignation** (xviii); **no borrowings of any kind**
   (ix(a), ii(b)); **no preferential allotment or private placement, ever** (x(b)); **no previously
   unrecorded income surrendered** (viii); no evergreening, nothing overdue beyond ninety days; **no
   promoter exists**, so no pledge, no promoter RPT and no promoter salary test has a subject.

### 5.3 Why not "High"

Three reasons, each sized:

1. **The single most-used cash metric is wrong by 78.7% as printed**, and the error was invisible to
   the doctrine's own triage screen — RF-033 records that the 8-point screen, run as designed, would
   have reported 1.269 and passed a test the correct number fails. A number that decides a gate,
   reported on the wrong side of parity, is not consistent with a High trust verdict even when the
   defect is classification rather than fabrication.
2. **78.51% of revenue has no audited disaggregation against a peer set that is 4 of 4**, and the ~70%
   equity-derivatives concentration that carries the entire case is attested only in the MD&A, on which
   no auditor has opined. Any durability judgement on that stream rests on unaudited evidence.
3. **Three quantified disclosures were removed from one annual report and two of the underlying items
   were growing.** The SEBI regulatory fee grew 70.8% in the year its note was deleted; the claims
   population rose 29.2% in the year its probability grading vanished; and the ₹10,600 crore guarantee's
   comparative was restated to ₹24 lakh with no stated basis. `DERIVED: three of six stopped
   disclosures cover live items` (`W3_notes.md` §4). Against that, FY2026 also **added** disclosure —
   a reasonable-assurance report on BRSR Core, a full discontinued-operation disposal table, and a new
   Clearing and Settlement footnote — so "reducing transparency" is too strong as a general
   characterisation and is not adopted. What survives is the pattern **in the subset**.

### 5.4 Why "Moderate" rather than "Low"

"Low" would mean the reported numbers survive but a valuation built on them carries substantial risk of
resting on a figure that moves. That is not the position, for one reason above all others:

> **The base rate that decides this gate: in ten years, zero rupees of realised loss at this company is
> attributable to an accounting-quality channel.**

The realised losses in the window are exhaustively identifiable and every one of them came from a
regulator, a counterparty or a capital-allocation decision:
₹1,861 lakh (FY2020 SEBI ISF/IPF plough-back) · ₹1,453 lakh (FY2021 S&P Dow Jones arbitration) ·
₹7,347 lakh or ₹22,421 lakh (FY2024–FY2025 retrospective SEBI levy — **contradiction unresolved**) ·
₹8.33 crore of SEBI penalties FY22–FY26, `DERIVED: 833 / 2,48,725 = 0.33%` of one year's PAT ·
₹1,535 lakh of receivable write-offs across three years, `1,535 / 2,49,698 = 0.61%` ·
₹20,919 lakh injected into India INX over ten years against ₹24,023 lakh of cumulative loss ·
and an entire segment extinguished by RBI's 5-Jan-2024 ETCD directions, currency-derivatives ADT
₹9,734 crore → nil.

**Not one of those is an accounting event.** No restatement of earnings beyond ₹2,256 lakh in FY2018.
No impairment of a capitalised item. No provision that had to be topped up. No qualification. No fraud
reported. No ADT-4. The channel through which this company has actually lost money is **the regulator**,
and the accounts are not where that risk lives.

The corollary is the discipline this verdict rests on: **a long register of striking findings is not
the same as a demonstrated cost.** Every disconfirming item recorded above was tested and is carried,
and where the register's framing did not survive the test — the receivable blow-out, the provision
reversal, the capex step-up, the "structural efficiency" as an L2 rather than L3 break, the levy
mismatch as a worsening rather than a level — **the narrower version is what ships.**

### FINDING: Reported profit at BSE is reliable in amount and reported operating cash flow is not usable as printed — and the whole of the difference is disclosed money wearing the wrong caption
S1 WHAT: The consolidated statement of cash flows reports a ten-year cumulative CFO of ₹8,24,646 lakh against cumulative PAT of ₹6,48,302 lakh, a conversion of 1.272. On an own-account basis — the same ten filings, with the Core SGF flows the company itself stopped presenting in FY2025 removed from every year and the clearing-member float removed throughout — the conversion is **0.712** `[AR FY2017, Note 18, PDF p170/pr168]` through `[AR FY26, Note 16, PDF p206/pr195]`. BSE consolidates a clearing corporation because SEBI requires it to own the clearing node, so member margin, settlement obligations and clearing-bank deposits move through group cash; and BSE collects Securities Transaction Tax as the government's agent, so the collection balance moves through it too. Under Ind AS 7 a movement in a current liability lands in operating working capital by default unless the preparer excludes it, and BSE excludes exactly two classes by footnote — the parent's earmarked balances since FY2018 and the subsidiaries' Core SGF since FY2025 — and excludes neither of these. S3 EVIDENCE: `DERIVED: 8,24,646 − 70,175 Core SGF add-backs − 2,92,958 float = 4,61,513; 4,61,513 / 6,48,302 = 0.712` against `8,24,646 / 6,48,302 = 1.272`, so the printed series is `1.272 / 0.712 = 1.787`, **78.7% too high**. DISCONFIRM: Sought hard, because a result this far from the headline invites confirmation bias, and four things cut the company's way. (i) **Nothing is concealed** — Note 16's three-way split exists in all ten annual reports and every year's (A)+(B)+(C) ties to that year's balance-sheet caption exactly; the disclosure SEBI requires is made in full. (ii) **The presentation is standard** — Ind AS 7 does not require collected taxes or member balances to be excluded from operating cash flow, most Indian filers present them the same way, and **no peer publishes an own-account CFO**, so this is an analyst construction and not a compliance finding. S3 EVIDENCE: The realised losses, exhaustively: ₹1,861 lakh (FY2020 SEBI ISF/IPF plough-back after a 2005–2017 inspection); ₹1,453 lakh (FY2021 S&P Dow Jones arbitration award); ₹7,347 lakh or ₹22,421 lakh (FY2024–FY2025 retrospective SEBI levy — **the contradiction between W3_cost and W3_notes/W3_revenue is unresolved and both figures stand**); ₹8.33 crore of SEBI penalties FY22–FY26, `DERIVED: 833 / 2,48,725 = 0.33%` of one year's consolidated PAT; ₹1,535 lakh of receivable write-offs across three years, `1,535 / 2,49,698 = 0.61%`; ₹20,919 lakh injected into India International Exchange across ten years against ₹24,023 lakh of cumulative loss and no dividend ever; and an entire segment extinguished by RBI's 5-Jan-2024 ETCD directions, currency-derivatives ADT ₹9,734 crore to nil. **The counter-proportion that makes this a finding rather than a clean bill:** against that list, the accounting-channel loss is **nil** — the twenty-six restatement movements W3_notes compiled across the corpus contain exactly two earnings restatements, one of them the mechanical bonus adjustment `DERIVED: 96.55 / 3 = 32.18`, and the other stated and quantified at ₹2,256 lakh, **−3.17% of FY2018 PAT**; nine consecutive unmodified audit opinions with one emphasis of matter quantified at ₹1,264 lakh and resolved; CARO 3(xi)(a) **no fraud noticed or reported**, 3(xi)(b) **no ADT-4 filed**, 3(xviii) **no auditor resignation**, 3(viii) **no previously unrecorded income surrendered**, 3(ix)(a) and 3(ii)(b) **no borrowings and no working-capital limits at any point in the year**.
S4 CONNECTS: Governance — the auditor is S.R. Batliboi & Co. LLP from FY2018 to a mandatory rotation cliff at FY2027 with no successor process disclosed, and a partner rotation (Jayesh Gandhi → Pikashoo Mutha) occurred at FY2025 without any change in opinion, so the clean record spans two partners; non-audit fees are trivial at ₹2 lakh, 2.3% of ₹88 lakh. Industry — every realised cost event in the list is regulator-authored, and three landed in three years, which is the demonstrated shape of this business's risk rather than an inference. Management commentary — W2_promise scores management's hit rate on 53 scoreable claims at **61.3%**, `DERIVED: (30 + 5 × 0.5) / 53 = 32.5 / 53`, with 55.0% on outcome claims and 65.2% on action claims, so the gap between what management says and what happens is a **commentary** problem and not a **books** problem, which is exactly the distinction this finding turns on. Business — BSE has no promoter and no controlling shareholder, so the single most common Indian accounting-loss channel has no subject here.
DOMAINS: Governance, Notes, IncomeStatement, Industry, MgmtCommentary, Business
DISCONFIRM: Sought deliberately, because a base rate of zero is the kind of result that invites complacency, and four things cut against it. (i) **Absence of a realised loss is not absence of exposure** — the guarantee restatement means the group's off-balance-sheet clearing obligation is no longer quantified anywhere, so a loss in that channel would be invisible until it crystallised. (ii) The window is a **rising** one: FY2026 PAT is 9.4× FY2017's, and accounting failures surface disproportionately in contractions, of which this corpus contains few. (iii) The auditor rotation cliff at FY2027 means the ten-year clean record was produced by one firm, and a successor's first year is where re-estimates typically appear. (iv) The disclosure record is deteriorating rather than improving on three named items, so the base rate measures the past and the direction of travel is the other way. Recorded in the company's favour and not discounted: the FY2024 CFO restatement **cut** reported CFO by ₹19,857 lakh, the FY2026 ECL re-estimate is **net conservative by ₹879 lakh**, the buildings life moved to the **shorter** figure, the headline EPS management leads with is the **lower** number, and the voluntary Core SGF policy **reduces** reported profit by roughly ₹11,279 lakh a year at policy rate. Five conservative errors is not the register of a company managing earnings.
S5 MEANS: A long register of striking findings is not the same as a demonstrated cost, and this is the specific discipline that separates the two: at BSE the accounting is where the *measurement* problems live and the regulator is where the *money* problems live, so a trust verdict that treated the register's length as evidence of unreliability would be answering a question the evidence does not support and would miss the exposure that has actually cost this company money three times in three years.
BASE-RATE: This finding is the base rate. Stated as a rate: **zero accounting-channel losses in ten annual reports covering ₹6,48,302 lakh of cumulative PAT.** In the wider Indian system the comparison that matters is that cash-flow misclassification and related-party disclosure are the two most common Financial Reporting Review Board findings against listed filers, and BSE exhibits the first (the eight-year associate-dividend omission, ₹18,100 lakh) and not the second (AOC-2 clean, Regulation 23 at 34.9% of threshold, related-party receivables 0.24% of the book) — and even the first has produced no loss, because the ₹18,100 lakh is in the bank and evidenced on the standalone every year.
S6 IMPLICATION: The earnings-quality gate passes at **Moderate** on this evidence, and the constraint W8 inherits is not a discount for unreliability but a substitution of inputs — own-account cash for reported cash, unaudited evidence weighting for the 70% concentration, and a peak-cycle flag on FY2026 — with the residual risk carried where it actually sits, in the regulatory channel rather than the accounting one. Monitorable with a date: the FY2027 audit, May 2027, which is the successor auditor's first year under mandatory rotation and the single most likely occasion for a re-estimate to surface. Next check: FY2027 annual report, auditor's report and Key Audit Matters. Thesis-breaker: any FY2027 restatement of an FY2026 earnings figure, or a first modified opinion, either of which would move this verdict a full grade.
SEVERITY: Medium
